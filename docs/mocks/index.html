Saturdaze mocks v2

Responsive web app first. Five screens, one primary action each. Resize the window: 720px swaps the bottom nav for a top bar, 1024px puts Saturday and Sunday side by side.

Core

  • WeekendCover photo, one day beside its map, numbered stops, travel legs
  • Ideas · ActivitiesPhoto-led cards, one filter row, Add to day
  • Ideas · FoodTop pick, family vote, lock it in
  • Ideas · EventsSaturday, Sunday, coming soon, your suggestion
  • Past weekendsCover photos; rate, favourite, rename, repeat, remix
  • FamilyMembers, commitments, likes, preferences, sign out
  • Family · who signs inOwner view: invited, signed-in and no-sign-in members
  • Family · member viewA member who is not the owner: who's in is read-only
  • Review submissionsAdmin queue: approve or reject

States

  • Weekend · emptyFirst run, nothing drafted yet
  • Weekend · generatingSkeleton rows while the planner works
  • Past · emptyNo finished weekends yet
  • Review · emptyQueue is clear

Auth

  • Sign inDefault and wrong-password states
  • Create accountFamily name, email, password strength, consent
  • Reset passwordRequest, check email, new password, done, expired
  • Accept inviteJoin a family with your own password, invite no longer works
  • Verify emailCheck your email, verifying, verified, expired

Public

  • LandingHero with a live miniature of the Weekend screen
  • LegalTerms and Privacy on one template

Admin

  • Admin · Sign inSame account; the gate for non-administrators
  • Admin · Photo healthCoverage per catalog, each figure a filter
  • Admin · PlacesSearch, filter, worst health first
  • Admin · Place photosTiles, previews, upload, URL, primary, remove
  • Admin · Review queueKeep, make primary or reject ingested photos
  • Admin · Ingestion skipsWhy a run left a photo out
  • Admin · Activity logWho changed which photo, and how
  • Admin · Email templatesSearch and filter by category and status
  • Admin · Email templateEditor with live preview, states and actions

Dialogs

  • DialogsEvery modal and sheet, rendered inline, D1 to D30 and admin AD1 to AD9
