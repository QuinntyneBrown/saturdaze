Terms of Service

Last updated 17 May 2026

The short version

Saturdaze suggests weekend plans. You decide what to do. Be kind with what you submit, keep your password to yourself, and you can leave any time.

  1. 1. Acceptance
  2. 2. Your account
  3. 3. Your family's information
  4. 4. Suggestions and submissions
  5. 5. Ending your account
  6. 6. Contact

1. Acceptance

By creating an account you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use Saturdaze. We may update the terms; when we do, the date above changes and the app tells you the next time you sign in.

2. Your account

One account owns one family. Keep your password private. If you think someone else has used your account, reset your password and tell us.

You must be at least 18 to create an account. Kids appear in a family profile because a parent put them there, and only that family can see them.

3. Your family's information

Names, ages, commitments, likes and past weekends exist so the planner can do its job. We do not sell them and we do not show them to other families. The Privacy Policy explains the details.

4. Suggestions and submissions

Weekend plans are suggestions. Check opening hours, prices and weather before you set out. Places change and we cannot promise a listing is still accurate.

When you suggest a local event, you confirm the details are true as far as you know and that you have the right to share them. We review every suggestion before other families see it, and we may edit or reject it without giving a reason.

5. Ending your account

You can delete your account from the Family screen. Your family profile and past weekends are removed within 30 days. We may close accounts that abuse the service or submit spam.

6. Contact

Questions about these terms go to hello@saturdaze.app.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 May 2026

The short version

We keep what the planner needs and nothing more. Your family's details stay with your family. No ads, no selling data, and you can delete everything from the Family screen.

  1. 1. What we collect
  2. 2. How we use it
  3. 3. Sharing
  4. 4. Your choices
  5. 5. Contact

1. What we collect

Your email address and a hashed password. Your family profile: first names, ages, home location, recurring commitments, likes and dislikes. The weekends we draft for you, your ratings and favourites. The events you suggest.

We also keep ordinary server logs for a short time so we can fix problems. They contain your IP address and the pages you opened, not the contents of your family profile.

2. How we use it

To draft weekends. Home location gives us weather and drive times. Ages filter activities. Commitments stay locked. Past ratings steer what we suggest next. If the Friday preview is on, we email you a draft at 6pm on Friday.

3. Sharing

Other families never see your profile. A share link shows a read-only weekend to anyone who has the link, for seven days, and you can turn it off. Approved event suggestions appear to nearby families with the event details only, never the name of the person who sent them.

We use a weather provider and a hosting provider. They see the minimum needed to do their job and are not allowed to use it for anything else.

4. Your choices

Edit or remove anyone in your family from the Family screen. Turn the Friday preview off there too. Deleting your account removes your family profile and past weekends within 30 days.

5. Contact

Privacy questions go to hello@saturdaze.app.
