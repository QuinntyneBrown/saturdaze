# 1. Getting Started

This chapter answers the basics: what Saturdaze is, how to create an account
and sign in, and what to expect once you're in.

## What Saturdaze does, in one sentence

Saturdaze turns "we have no plan this weekend" into a ready-to-use Saturday
and Sunday for your family, in about one tap.

## What you get

When you plan a weekend, Saturdaze hands you:

- A **timeline for Saturday** and a **timeline for Sunday**, from morning
  through evening
- The fixed stuff already in place (swim, church, your workout) as
  immovable blocks
- **Activity ideas** picked for your kids' ages, the weather, and the drive
- **Restaurant suggestions** that fit your family — including the picky
  eaters
- **Drive blocks** to and from each outing, so you see the real time cost
- **Downtime blocks**, on purpose, so the day doesn't feel like a forced
  march
- A **heads-up panel** that points out useful things ("rain moves in
  Sunday afternoon")

You can take the whole plan as-is, regenerate a day or the whole weekend,
lock the blocks you love so they survive a reshuffle, or hand the plan off
to your calendar.

## What it does *not* do

A few things to set expectations:

- It is **not a generic calendar**. It only knows about the fixed
  commitments you enter in your profile.
- It is **not a booking engine**. It recommends restaurants and activities,
  but reservations and tickets are still on you.
- It does **not** edit a day block-by-block yet. You shape a day by
  **regenerating** it and **locking** the parts you want to keep — there's
  no per-block "swap" or "drag to reorder" in the app today.

## Creating your account

Saturdaze is a sign-in app, so the very first thing is an account.

1. Open the app. The first screen is a short **marketing/splash** page with
   a **Get started** (sign-up) button and a **Sign in** link. You can also
   tap **See a sample weekend** to look around before committing.
2. Tap **Get started** to open the **sign-up** form and fill in:
   - **Family name** (e.g. "The Browns")
   - **Home location** (e.g. "Port Credit, Mississauga, ON")
   - **Email**
   - **Password** — at least **8 characters**
   - Agree to the **Terms** and **Privacy Policy**
   - Optionally tick **Friday preview** to opt into a Friday heads-up
3. Submit. Saturdaze sends a **verification email** and takes you to a
   **Check your email** screen.
4. Open the email and follow the link to **verify your address**. (If it
   doesn't arrive, you can request a fresh one.)

> **Already have an account?** Tap **Sign in** on the splash screen instead.

## Signing in

1. From the splash screen, tap **Sign in**.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Leave **Remember me** on if this is your own device — Saturdaze keeps you
   signed in across app restarts. Turn it off on a shared device, and you'll
   be signed out when the session ends.
4. Tap **Sign in**. You land on **This Weekend**.

Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen, enter
your email, and follow the reset link that's emailed to you.

To **sign out**, go to the **Family** tab (Profile) and tap **Sign out** in
the account block at the bottom.

## The four areas of the app

Once you're signed in, the bottom of the screen always shows four tabs:

| Tab          | What lives here                                          |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Weekend**  | This weekend's plan, weather, heads-up, and quick actions |
| **Discover** | A curated grid of activities near you                   |
| **Saved**    | Past weekends you can remix or repeat                   |
| **Family**   | Your family setup, preferences, and your account        |

A handful of screens open in context rather than from a tab — the
**Itinerary** for a single day (tap a day card on the home screen), and the
**Restaurants**, **Events**, and **Errand** screens. Each later chapter says
how to reach the screen it covers.
