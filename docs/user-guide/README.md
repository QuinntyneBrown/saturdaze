# Saturdaze User Guide

Welcome! Saturdaze is a family weekend planner. You tap one button, and it
hands you back a ready-to-use Saturday and Sunday — fixed commitments in
place, activities picked for your kids' ages and the weather, lunch and
dinner suggestions, drive times, and downtime built in.

This guide walks you through the app the way you actually use it, step by
step, in plain language. It describes **what the app does today** — where a
feature is planned but not yet wired up, the guide says so plainly rather
than pretending.

## Who Saturdaze is for

Saturdaze is built for families who:

- Have kids of mixed ages (think 5 to 12) and an early-evening wind-down
- Live in or near Port Credit, Mississauga
- Already have fixed weekend commitments (swimming, church, a workout)
- Like parks, hikes, the Zoo, the Rec Room, live theatre, short drives,
  and lavender fields
- Want suggestions, not a blank calendar
- Find the "what's for lunch?" debate exhausting

The app ships pre-loaded with the Brown family as the worked example, so the
screenshots and sample data you see are theirs.

## The shape of the app

Saturdaze is a sign-in app. Once you're in, day-to-day navigation lives in a
**bottom bar with four tabs**:

| Tab          | Where it goes      | What lives there                              |
| ------------ | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Weekend**  | This Weekend (home) | The plan, weather, heads-up, quick actions   |
| **Discover** | Activities          | A curated grid of things to do near you      |
| **Saved**    | Saved weekends      | Past plans you can remix or repeat           |
| **Family**   | Family profile      | Your household setup and your account        |

A few screens sit outside those four tabs and open in context — the
**Itinerary** (tap a day card), the **Restaurants**, **Events**, and
**Errand** screens. Each chapter tells you how to reach the screen it covers.

## A note on names and labels

Screen names match what you see at the top of each page — for example
**This Weekend**, **Family profile**, **Discover**, and **Food**. When a
button or card is mentioned in **bold**, that's exactly what its label says
in the app.
