# 3. Planning Your Weekend

This is the part that does the magic. In this chapter we go from a blank
weekend to a complete plan in about a minute.

The home screen — labelled **This Weekend** and reached with the **Weekend**
tab — is where it all happens.

## Step 1: Open the app and read the room

When you land on **This Weekend**, the top of the screen greets you and
shows the **weather strip**: Saturday and Sunday side by side, each with a
high, a low, and a short note. Glance at this first — it usually tells you
whether you're looking at an indoor or outdoor weekend before you tap
anything. The planner reads the same forecast, giving indoor activities a
nudge on wet days and outdoor ones a nudge when it's sunny and warm.

## Step 2: Tap "Plan This Weekend"

The big button in the hero says **Plan This Weekend**. Tap it.

Saturdaze plans the **upcoming weekend** (the next Saturday and Sunday). In
a few seconds you'll see two **day cards** appear — one for Saturday, one
for Sunday — each showing:

- The **day** and its **weather**
- The **day's highlight** (the standout outing)
- A few small **chips** summarizing the day

That's your weekend at a glance. If both cards look reasonable, you can stop
here — you have a plan.

> **Re-tapping is safe.** Planning the same weekend again just returns the
> plan you already have rather than throwing it away and starting over. To
> get fresh ideas on purpose, use **Regenerate** (below).

## Step 3: Read the "A heads-up" panel

Below the day cards is **A heads-up** — short notices Saturdaze surfaces
about the weekend (weather turning, something worth knowing). Each can carry
a small action button. Skim it, act on what's useful, ignore the rest.

## Step 4: Open a day to see the timeline

Tap either **day card** to open that day's full **Itinerary** — a timeline
from morning through evening, broken into blocks (commitments, activities,
meals, drives, and downtime). For everything you can do there, see
[The Itinerary](04-the-itinerary.md).

## Step 5: Use the Quick actions

Scroll down to **Quick actions** on the home screen. There are three:

- **Regenerate the weekend** — same commitments, fresh ideas. Confirm in the
  dialog and the whole weekend re-plans. Anything you've locked is kept.
- **Lock what's already perfect** — turns on **lock mode**. Tap the blocks
  you love to pin them, then regenerate so they survive the shuffle. (More on
  locking in [The Itinerary](04-the-itinerary.md).)
- **Share this weekend** — builds a read-only share link and opens a share
  sheet with a tidy summary you can copy or send on.

There's also a **calendar** option that gives you subscription links —
download an `.ics` file, subscribe via `webcal`, or add to Google Calendar —
so the plan lands in the calendar you already use.

## What if you don't like the plan?

A few options, from smallest to biggest:

1. **Regenerate one day.** Open the day's Itinerary and tap **Regenerate** —
   only that day reshuffles; the other stays put.
2. **Lock the keepers, then regenerate.** Turn on lock mode from
   **Quick actions**, pin the blocks that work, then regenerate the weekend.
   Locked blocks stay; everything else is fair game.
3. **Regenerate the whole weekend.** From **Quick actions**, tap
   **Regenerate the weekend** to redo both days at once.

> **What you can't do (yet):** there's no per-block "swap this one activity"
> or "drag to reorder" inside a day. You shape a day by locking the good
> parts and regenerating the rest.

## A typical first-weekend flow

1. Open the app, read the forecast.
2. Tap **Plan This Weekend**.
3. Read both day cards. Like Saturday, unsure about Sunday.
4. Open Sunday → **Regenerate**.
5. Get a better Sunday. Turn on lock mode, lock Saturday's keepers.
6. Tap **Share this weekend** (or add it to your calendar).
7. Done — under two minutes.
