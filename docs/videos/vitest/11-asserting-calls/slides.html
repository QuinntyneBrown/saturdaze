Pitfalls

  • A bare toHaveBeenCalled when you know the arguments
  • toHaveBeenCalledWith when order matters
  • Nth counts from 1; mock.calls from 0
  • Sharing a mock across tests without mockClear
  • mockReset when you only meant to clear

Things to remember
