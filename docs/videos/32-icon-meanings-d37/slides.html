D37: one icon, one meaning

True arrows, and a bookmark for the primary photo, so the star only means a rating

What drifted

star

Rating and Make primary

Past tab and rating stars in the family app; Make primary and the Primary chip in Admin

arrow_right · chevron_right

The same glyph

M9 6l6 6-6 6 for both, and for arrow_left

Why it matters

  • Close to WCAG 3.2.4 consistent identification: same job, same look; different job, different look
  • A shape that sometimes means "rate" and sometimes "promote" makes people stop and check
  • A redraw of one icon should not change every row that shares it
