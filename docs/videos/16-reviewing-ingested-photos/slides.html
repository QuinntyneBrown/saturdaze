Things to remember

Provider photos arrive unreviewed and wait in the queue.
Keep and Make primary review and lock; Make primary states the cover impact.
Reject deletes the photo and remembers its address; ingestion skips it and says so.
Ingestion photo skips: every run's skips, read-only, linked.
Seeding and ingestion never undo an administrator's decision.
