# 44 · Meet Saturdaze

## Introduction

Saturdaze plans your family's weekend. Two days, already planned, around the things that never move and the weather that might.

## The weekend

Every Friday you get a draft of Saturday and Sunday. Each day is a timeline, hour by hour, with the drive between every stop and a map right beside it.

## Anchors

Tell Saturdaze who is in the family, their ages and what they love. Then lock what does not move: swim lessons, church, the weekly workout. The plan goes around them.

## Ideas

Ideas stay close to home. Parks, places to eat and local events, each with its drive time. Like one? Add it to the day.

## Make it yours

Not feeling it? Swap a stop for something else, or lock a day you love so it stays exactly as it is.

## Share and look back

Send the weekend to the grandparents with a share link. And every weekend you plan is kept, so you can rate it, repeat it or remix it.
