Design drift · Video 27

D05: soft text on wells

Grey #6B7280 was 4.22:1 on wells; secondary text is now #5B6270 at 5.35:1

What drifted

--sd-ink:         #1F2937;
--sd-ink-soft:    #6B7280;
--sd-ink-faint:   #636A77;
  • Mocks: chips, well bodies, resting segments, subtitles, labels, metadata
  • Product: --colorNeutralForeground2: #6b7280
  • Design system: logged as D05, fixed on its own side

Why it matters: WCAG 1.4.3 (AA)

Before

#6B7280

4.83 / 4.52 / 4.22:1

White / canvas / wells; fails 4.5:1 on wells

After

#5B6270

6.13 / 5.74 / 5.35:1

White / canvas / wells; passes AA everywhere

Three text strengths, in order

  • Foreground1 #1F2937: content
  • Foreground2 #5B6270: labels, metadata, chips, segments
  • Foreground3 #636A77: hints, placeholders, durations, footers (D04)
