D36: the forecast, in words

The weather disc is decorative; the day meta line now says Sunny, Cloudy, Rain, Snow, Warm or Cold

What drifted

Before

17 May · 22° / 14° · 3 stops · 1 h 36 min driving

The sun disc is aria-hidden; no word says "sunny"

Design system

17 May · Sunny · 22° / 14° · …

A weather word in the day meta line

Why it matters

  • WCAG 1.1.1 non-text content: an icon cannot be the only carrier
  • WCAG 1.4.1 use of colour: shape and colour are not enough
  • The fix costs one short word in a line that already exists

1 · Requirement, then mocks

L2-135

A day header writes the forecast as one word before the temperatures: Sunny, Cloudy, Rain, Snow, Warm or Cold. No forecast, no word and no temperatures.
