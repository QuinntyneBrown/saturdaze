D03: a focus ring you could barely see

Coral #E07856 was 2.81:1 on the cream canvas; the ring is now #A04B2C at 5.56:1

What drifted

--sd-focus-ring: 2px solid var(--sd-primary);
.field__input:focus-visible { outline: none; border-color: var(--sd-primary); ... }
  • Mocks: every ring and focused field border on --sd-primary #E07856
  • Product: --colorStrokeFocus2: #e07856
  • Design system: logged as D03, fixed on its own side

Why it matters: WCAG 1.4.11 and 2.4.7 (AA)

Before

#E07856 on cream #FAF7F2

2.81:1

Fails 1.4.11 (needs 3:1)

After

#A04B2C on cream #FAF7F2

5.56:1

5.18:1 on wells; passes AA

No new colour

  • #A04B2C is brand ramp step 70
  • Already the coral text on the soft tint (colorBrandForeground2)
  • The brand coral #E07856 keeps the mark, underlines and icons

Things to remember

  • A focus ring needs 3:1 against what it sits on
  • colorStrokeFocus2 draws every focus ring
  • Change the TypeScript theme, then regenerate
  • Mocks first, failing test, then delete the drift record
