D40: a stop that says "Stop"

Visually hidden "Stop " before the number instead of an aria-label on a <span>

What drifted

<span class="block__disc block__disc--num" aria-label="Stop 2">2</span>
  • A span has no role; most screen readers ignore its aria-label
  • Heard: just "2". The word "Stop" was lost

Why it matters

  • WCAG 1.3.1 info and relationships; 4.1.2 name, role, value
  • A name that assistive technology ignores is not a name
  • Text is read, searchable and robust; the disc looks the same

1 · Requirement, then mock

L2-137

A numbered stop disc carries its meaning as text: a visually hidden "Stop " before the visible number. No aria-label on a non-interactive element.

<span class="block__disc block__disc--num"><span class="visually-hidden">Stop </span>2</span>
