Design drift · Video 37

D42: landmarks that say what they are

Footer, pager, list and account button, named for assistive technology

What drifted

Footer

Inside <main>: generic, not contentinfo

Pager

A <div> in the mocks; labelled "Pages" in the app

Places list

A <div> of links in the mock

Avatar

A link in the mock; no aria-haspopup in the app

Why it matters

  • WCAG 1.3.1 info and relationships; 4.1.2 name, role, value
  • Landmarks are how screen reader users skip around; a footer inside main is not one
  • A button that opens a menu should say so; nothing changes visually

1 · Requirement, then mocks

L2-138

Footer = contentinfo outside main. Pager = navigation "Pagination". Lists are lists. Avatar = button with aria-haspopup="menu".

Mocks: footers after </main>, admin Places as <ul>, pagers as <nav>, avatar as <button> on 15 pages
