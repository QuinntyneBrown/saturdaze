Design drift · Video 34

D39: signing out is not dangerous

The admin gate's Sign out becomes a quiet warn-text button, as on Family

What drifted

Admin gate

Danger button

btn--danger btn--lg btn--block: solid brick red

Family page

Quiet warn-text

btn--quiet btn--warn-text: the same action, calm

Why it matters

  • Danger is reserved for confirming a destructive action in a dialog
  • Signing out destroys nothing: sign back in and it is all there
  • Same action, same look across the apps (cf. WCAG 3.2.4 consistent identification)
