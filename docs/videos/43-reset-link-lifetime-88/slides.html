The screens said 30 minutes; the server allowed 60. Now both say 60.

What the screens promised, and what the server did

Screens and mock

30 minutes

"The link works for 30 minutes." · "Reset links last 30 minutes."

Server and design

60 minutes

now.AddMinutes(60) in ForgotPasswordCommandHandler

L2-005 said nothing about expiry.

Why a mismatch on a security link matters

  • People give up, or ask again, while the link still works
  • The real window is twice what anyone was told
  • A control needs one number shared by users and reviewers
