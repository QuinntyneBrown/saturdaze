The choices

  1. No fifth navigation item. A date is part of a weekend (ADR-025, ADR-005).
  2. Every edit is a dialog. No inline forms, as everywhere in Saturdaze.
  3. The weekend sees one locked block. Regenerate, swap, lock and the agents keep it with no new code.
  4. Says who needs care, books nothing. No restaurant, activity or childcare.
  5. When nothing fits, it says why. Never an invented place.

Things to remember

  • "Plan a date" on Weekend: a meal out with a nearby activity, around the couple's commitments
  • The family weekend plans around it and says when the kids need care
  • Every change is a dialog; every pick has a reason; nothing is booked
