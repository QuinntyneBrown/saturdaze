D02: a primary button you could not read

White on coral #E07856 was 3.00:1; the fill is now #BF5130 at 4.74:1

What drifted

  --sd-primary:      #E07856;   /* warm coral — the "Plan This Weekend" CTA */
  --sd-primary-ink:  #FFFFFF;
  • Mocks: .btn--primary on --sd-primary
  • Product: --colorBrandBackground: #e07856
  • Design system: logged as D02, fixed on its own side

Why it matters: WCAG 1.4.3 (AA)

Before

White on #E07856

3.00:1

Fails for 14px text (needs 4.5:1)

After

White on #BF5130

4.74:1

Passes AA

The brand coral stays

  • #E07856 keeps the brand mark
  • Current-page underline, focus ring, coral icons
  • Rule: nothing is ever written on it
