Cover photo

The cover opens a weekend with one photo of its best moment. The dates, the weekend's name and a one-line summary sit on a dark gradient at the bottom; the family can change the photo, and the weekend's actions sit right under it.

Overview

Use the cover once per page, at the top of a single weekend (Weekend, and a past weekend opened from Past). It replaces the page header there: its title is the page's <h1>. The same photo appears on the weekend's Past card and on the link the family shares.

  • Use media for photos inside cards and rows; the cover is the only full-width photo.
  • "Change photo" (.cover__edit) opens dialog D29 (Cover photo), a photo pick of the weekend's places plus "Your own photo".
  • The weekend's actions (Add to calendar, Share, More) go in .cover-actions directly below, never on the photo.

The examples use docs/mocks/images/*.svg, which are illustrated placeholders and must not ship (docs/mocks/README.md).

Anatomy

  1. Photo.cover__img, cropped with object-fit: cover; loaded eagerly with high fetch priority, since it is above the fold.
  2. Credit.media__credit, moved to the top left inside a cover so it never collides with the title.
  3. Change photo.btn.btn--quiet.btn--sm.cover__edit, top right on a translucent surface.
  4. Eyebrow.cover__eyebrow: the date range, 12px uppercase.
  5. Title.cover__title: the weekend's name, --typographyTitle1. The page's <h1>.
  6. Summary and gradient.cover__sub on .cover__body, a gradient from --colorBackgroundOverlayStrong to transparent.

Variants

One component, three situations: a photo chosen from the weekend's places (credited to its source), the family's own photo ("Your photo"), and no photo yet.

Sizes

The cover has one size: the full width of the content container, with a ratio that changes at the 720px breakpoint. Under 720px it is 16:9, so a phone shows enough photo above the title; from 720px it is 21:8, a wide band that leaves the day columns above the fold. Resize this page to see the examples switch.

States

The cover itself is not interactive. Its states are the photo's (loading, no photo) and the Change photo button's, forced here with data-state.

Both themes

  • Loading: the frame keeps its ratio and the neutral well shows while the photo arrives; the title and summary render at once on the gradient, so the page is readable before the photo is.
  • Saving: after "Use this photo" in dialog D29, the dialog closes and the new photo replaces the old one when it has loaded; the button shows aria-busy in the meantime and a polite status announces "Cover photo updated".
  • Hover on Change photo turns its translucent surface solid; it applies only under (hover: hover).

Responsive behaviour

  • Ratio: 16:9 under 720px, 21:8 from 720px (45rem). object-fit: cover crops the sides on desktop and the top and bottom on phones, so choose photos with the subject in the middle.
  • Body padding grows from 16px to 24px at the sides at 720px. The summary is capped at the reading measure (--layoutMeasure).
  • Long titles wrap to two lines; at 360px with a 16:9 cover, a title over two lines starts to cover the photo's middle. Keep names short (see Content).
  • Actions (.cover-actions) wrap and stay right-aligned; under 380px the icon-only More button keeps them on one row.
  • Serve a srcset: 720px wide for phones, 1600px for desktop; the cover is the page's largest contentful paint.

Theming

Text on the cover uses fixed light-on-dark tokens in both themes (--colorNeutralForegroundStaticInverted on --colorBackgroundOverlayStrong), because the photo decides the background, not the theme. The well behind a loading photo and the Change photo surface follow the theme.

/* "Change photo" floats on the photo: translucent, solid on hover */
.cover__edit { --sd-btn-bg: var(--colorNeutralBackgroundAlpha); --sd-btn-bg-hover: var(--colorNeutralBackground1); }

To re-skin, override the button's component tokens or the body padding; do not lighten the gradient, it is what keeps the title readable.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <figure> with a <figcaption> holding the date, the page's <h1> and the summary. No ARIA pattern applies. "Change photo" is a link to the dialog route (or a <button> that opens dialog D29 following the APG Dialog (Modal) pattern).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabChange photo, then the actions row (Add to calendar, Share, More) in DOM order.
EnterOpens dialog D29; on close, focus returns to Change photo.

Focus

Change photo shows the shared ring (--colorStrokeFocus2). On a dark photo the ring's contrast depends on the photo; the 2px offset shows the photo, and the button's own light surface keeps the control itself visible.

Labelling

  • The photo's alt describes the photo ("Rows of lavender running to the horizon at Terre Bleu Lavender Farm"); the title names the weekend, so they do not repeat each other.
  • When the family's own photo has no description, use alt="" and let the caption carry the meaning; never use the file name.
  • The no-photo tile is decorative (aria-hidden="true"); its button says "Add photo".

Contrast

Title and summary on the gradient (worst case, over white)
Credit on its pill
Change photo label on its hover surface
Change photo border on its surface

The text block starts 40px into the gradient, so the eyebrow never sits on its transparent end. Check very bright photos (snow, white sand) with the product's contrast check before choosing them as defaults.

Motion

None on the cover. The Change photo button's 1px hover lift and tint stop under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Change photo is 32px tall and sits 12px in from the corner, clear of the credit; the actions row is 40px. Both exceed the 24px minimum.

Content

  • Eyebrow: the date range with an en dash and spaces, "17 – 18 May"; add the year for past weekends ("10 – 11 May 2026").
  • Title: "This weekend" for the current one; a short name for past ones ("Bronte Creek + Rec Room"), at most about 30 characters.
  • Summary: one sentence a parent would say, weather first for an upcoming weekend ("Sunny Saturday for the lavender, a cloudy Sunday for the Rec Room") and highlights for a past one.
  • Button: "Change photo" when there is one, "Add photo" when there is not.
  • Credit: as on media: "Photo · Terre Bleu", "Your photo".
