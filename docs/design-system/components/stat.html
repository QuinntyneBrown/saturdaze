Component · Data display

Stat

A stat card answers one admin question with one figure: how many activities, restaurants and upcoming events families can recognise at a glance. The figure leads, the bar shows the share, and every count underneath opens the list behind it.

Overview

Use a stat on an admin dashboard when one number tells the team whether to act. Photo health is the reference: three cards, one per kind of place, each saying how many have a primary photo that shows. Families never see stat cards; their pages speak in plans, not figures.

  • Figure is always "part of whole" (<strong>16</strong> of 20), never a bare percentage. The whole stays visible so 80% of 5 does not read like 80% of 500.
  • Bar repeats the share for a quick scan across cards. Its tone follows the kind of place: leaf for activities, sun for restaurants, sky for events, brand when the stat is not about a kind of place.
  • Link list breaks the gap down into reasons. Every row opens the Places list already filtered, so the next step is one click.
  • Delta (optional) compares with the last period. It always carries a sign and an arrow as well as colour.

Use a progress bar when the share is moving toward done (an upload, an ingestion run), a badge or chip for a single count inside another component, and a table when the team needs to compare more than three figures.

Anatomy

  1. LabelThe card's heading (<h2> on the dashboard), --typographyBody2 semibold, uppercase, wide tracking, --colorNeutralForeground2. Names the card for aria-labelledby.
  2. Figure--typographyLargeTitle, tabular numerals. The part is <strong> in --colorNeutralForeground1; "of 20" stays secondary.
  3. SubWhat is being counted, one short line, --typographyBody2.
  4. Bar and fill8px track (--colorNeutralBackground3 with a 1px --colorNeutralStroke2 edge), pill fill in the tone colour, width from --sd-progress. role="img" with the percentage as its name.
  5. Count link.stat__link led by a .chip--count, 40px tall, divided by a hairline. Hover turns the text brand.
  6. ContainerA card with .card--pad-lg (20px) and an 8px gap between parts.

Loading

While the figures load, the card keeps its label and shape and shows skeletons for the figure, sub and bar. The card is aria-busy="true" until every figure has arrived, so a screen reader does not read half a dashboard.

  • Zero rows stay in the list so the three cards line up row for row; the link still opens the filtered list, which then shows its empty state.
  • Error: if one figure fails to load, that card shows an inline alert ("Could not load restaurants. Try again") in place of the figure; the other cards stay.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 1024px .stat-grid is one column with a 16px gap: each card reads top to bottom on a phone or a narrow admin window.
  • From 1024px it is three equal columns with a 24px gap, so the same row of each list sits at the same height across cards.
  • The figure never scales down. If "1,204 of 1,380" does not fit a third of the grid, the stat belongs in a table.
  • The sub and link text wrap; the count chip stays at the start of the first line.

Theming

The bar and fill share their rules with the progress bar: the fill reads --sd-progress-fill and its width reads --sd-progress. A tone modifier only sets --sd-progress-fill, so a new tone is one line and never touches .stat__fill.

/* A new tone for a future "Stays" category */
.stat__fill--indoor { --sd-progress-fill: var(--colorPaletteIndoorForeground3); }

In the dark theme the card is the raised umber surface, the fills switch to the lighter palette steps and the delta colours use their dark-theme foregrounds; all pairs below are checked in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each card is a <section aria-labelledby> named by its label, so it is a region a screen reader can jump to. The bar is role="img" with the percentage as its name; it is a static measure, not a progressbar. The count links are plain links in a list. No APG widget pattern applies.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the count links, card by card, in reading order.
EnterOpens the Places list filtered to that reason.

Focus

The shared 2px --colorStrokeFocus2 ring wraps the whole row, chip included, with the small radius.

Labelling

Leave a space between the count chip and the text (<span class="chip chip--count">4</span> without a photo) so the link is named "4 without a photo", not "4without a photo". The same phrase appears on every card; its card heading gives it context (2.4.4). Add the category in visually hidden text if the links are ever listed outside the card.

Motion

The fill grows to its width over --durationNormal when a figure updates. Under prefers-reduced-motion the token is zeroed and the fill jumps to its new width.

Touch

Count links are 40px tall (--controlHeightMedium) and full width, well above the 24px minimum, with a hairline between them.

Content

  • Label: the kind of thing, plural, two words at most: "Activities", "Upcoming events".
  • Figure: "part of whole". No percentage in the figure; the bar and its name carry it.
  • Sub: what was counted, starting lowercase so it reads on from the figure: "with a primary photo that shows".
  • Link rows: the reason in plain words, singular after the number: "4 without a photo", "1 blocked URL", "2 missing alt text".
  • Delta: sign, number and period: "+6 since last week". Never "up 6" without a period.
