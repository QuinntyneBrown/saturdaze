Progress bar

A progress bar shows how far a task has got when it takes more than a couple of seconds: a photo uploading, an admin ingestion run checking places. It says what is happening, how much is done, and whether it failed.

Overview

Use a progress bar when a task takes longer than about two seconds and the person waits for it on this screen. Three components share one 8px track:

  • Progress (.progress): a task moving toward done. Determinate when the total is known (bytes uploaded, places checked); indeterminate (.progress--indeterminate) until it is.
  • Strength meter (.strength): three segments and a sentence under a new password, on create account and reset password.
  • Stat bar (.stat__bar): a static share on an admin stat card. It is a measure, not progress, so it is role="img".

For a short wait with no measurable steps, use a spinner (the weekend planner's "Working through your locks…" status row). For content that is loading into place, use a skeleton. See feedback and loading.

Anatomy

  1. LabelWhat is happening, --typographyBody2. Names the progressbar through aria-labelledby.
  2. Value.progress__value: percentage or "3 of 42", tabular numerals, --colorNeutralForeground2. Hidden for indeterminate.
  3. Fill.progress__fill: pill, width from --sd-progress, colour from --sd-progress-fill.
  4. Track.progress__bar: 8px, --colorNeutralBackground3 with a 1px --colorNeutralStroke2 inner edge.

Stat bar

The same track, static, on the stat card.

Sizes

One size. The track is 8px everywhere; the strength meter's segments are 4px. Width follows the container.

States

Progress has no pointer or focus states; it is not interactive. Its states are how far the task has got.

  • Complete stays for a moment with "Done" or the final count, then the bar is replaced by the result (the uploaded photo, a toast "Photo sent for review.").
  • Failed keeps the fill where it stopped, turns it danger, and puts the reason and a retry next to it: an inline message in a dialog, or a toast when the dialog has closed.
  • Indeterminate switches to determinate as soon as the total is known; it never runs for more than a few seconds without text that says what it is waiting for.

Responsive behaviour

  • The bar fills its container at every width; it never has a fixed width.
  • In .progress__head the label wraps and the value keeps its place at the end. Long file names wrap rather than truncate, so the person can tell two uploads apart.
  • The strength meter matches its password field's width; its sentence wraps under the segments.

Theming

The fill reads --sd-progress-fill (colour) and --sd-progress (width); .progress--brand, --danger and the stat tones only set the colour. The track is shared with .stat__bar. In the dark theme the track is the umber well, the fills switch to their lighter steps, and the strength segments follow the status ramps. Dark brand and danger fills are close in hue, which is one more reason the failed state always says "Failed" in words.

/* Width is data, not a class: set it from the template */
<span class="progress__fill" [style.--sd-progress.%]="percent"></span>

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The track is role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax; add aria-valuetext when a count reads better than a percentage ("31 of 42 places", "Failed at 64%"). Indeterminate bars omit aria-valuenow. A native <progress> is equivalent if the product prefers it. The strength meter is not a progressbar: its sentence is the content, linked to the password field with aria-describedby. Static shares (stat bar) are role="img".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the bar; it is not focusable. Cancel or Try again next to it are ordinary buttons.

Focus

Progress never takes focus. When an upload finishes inside a dialog, focus stays where it was; the result is announced through a status region.

Labelling

Name the bar with its visible label (aria-labelledby). Announce milestones, not every percent: put "Uploading memorial-park.jpg", "Uploaded" and "Upload failed" in a role="status" region, and leave the bar's value for people who ask for it. The strength sentence is aria-live="polite" in the mocks; announce only when the level changes, not on every key press.

Motion

The fill's width eases over --durationNormal. The indeterminate fill sweeps across the track every --durationShimmer. Under prefers-reduced-motion those durations are zeroed, so instead of flickering the fill holds still at full width in a quieter brand step, and the label carries the "still working" message.

Touch

Not a target. Keep Cancel or Try again next to the bar at the 32px small button size or larger.

Content

  • Label with a verb in the present while running, the past when done: "Uploading memorial-park.jpg" → "Uploaded".
  • Use the unit the person cares about: "31 of 42" places for an ingestion run, a percentage for a file.
  • Failure says where it stopped and why, next to the bar: "Upload stopped at 64%" and "The connection dropped. Try again."
  • Strength: the level, a middle dot, and the one change that would raise it: "OK · eight characters or more. Add a capital letter to make it strong."
