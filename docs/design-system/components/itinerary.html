Itinerary

The itinerary is the heart of the Weekend page: one column per day, read top to bottom like a fridge schedule. Each block says when, how long and what; numbered stops match the map; travel legs say how far the next drive is.

Overview

Use the itinerary to show one planned day in time order. It is made of four parts that only appear together:

  • Day (.day): a section with a sticky header (weather, day name, date and forecast, stop count and driving time, Regenerate and Lock day) and an ordered list.
  • Block (.block): one thing the family does. Time and duration on the left, a disc on the rail, the title and one line of why, optional chips and photo, and actions.
  • Leg (.leg): the drive between two blocks, dotted on the rail. Legs over 10 minutes carry a Directions link.
  • Ghost row (.ghost-row): the dashed "Add an errand" row after the list.

Blocks at home (breakfast, quiet time, bath and books) show an icon disc. Blocks the family drives to are stops, numbered 1, 2, 3 in the same order as the pins on the map. For a single place outside a day, use a card or a list row instead.

Anatomy

  1. Day headerWeather disc, title (--typographyTitle2), meta line, day actions. Sticky on the page surface with a hairline under it.
  2. TimeStart time (.block__clock, semibold, tabular) over duration (.block__dur, --colorNeutralForeground3). 3.5rem column, right aligned.
  3. Rail and disc1px rail through a 32px disc. The disc shows an icon, or the stop number in brand. A 4px halo in the surface colour cuts the rail.
  4. BodyTitle (--typographySubtitle1), one clamped line of why, optional chips.
  5. Thumbnail (optional)72 × 54px photo of the place in a media row; decorative (alt=""), the title names it.
  6. Actions or chevronFrom 720px: Why this, Swap, Lock as small ghost icon buttons. Under 720px: a chevron link to the block's details.
  7. Travel legDotted rail, car icon, minutes and kilometres, Directions link over 10 minutes.

  • Selected (.block--active) marks the block whose pin is active on the map. It is a tint, so the matching pin also gets its own ring and the block's position is announced by its stop number.
  • Done (.block--done) is for errands ticked off during the weekend. The title is struck through and muted, the disc goes grey, and the sub line says "Done" too, so the state never rests on colour alone.
  • Locked blocks and commitments show a chip with a lock icon and a word, never the green disc alone.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 720px the day header sticks to the top of the screen. Day buttons are 36px icon-only squares (.day__btn-label hidden), and every block ends in a 32px chevron link to its details dialog; the row actions are not rendered.
  • From 720px the header sticks under the 64px top bar (--layoutTopBarHeight) and the day buttons show their labels. Chevrons go; Why this, Swap and Lock appear on hover and on focus inside the row.
  • Touch screens from 720px (hover: none, a tablet) always show the actions; there is no hover to reveal them (D21).
  • Planner: one day shows at a time behind the Saturday / Sunday segments. Under 1024px the map sits above the day; from 1024px it sits beside it, sticky.
  • Titles wrap; the sub line is clamped to one line (.clamp-1). The time column and rail never shrink, so times always line up.

Theming

A block reads three component tokens: --sd-block-disc-bg, --sd-block-disc-fg and --sd-block-halo. Kinds (--commitment, --locked, --errand) set the disc pair; hover and --active set the halo to the tint so the rail still stops cleanly at the disc. The numbered disc overrides the pair with the brand ink.

/* An itinerary on a card instead of the page canvas */
.card .block { --sd-block-halo: var(--colorNeutralBackground1); }
.card .day__header { background: var(--colorNeutralBackground1); }

In the dark theme the rail and hairlines use the warm umber strokes, the stop disc turns light coral with a dark numeral, and the leg link uses the lighter sky step.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each day is a <section aria-labelledby> named by its <h2>; the blocks and legs are items of one <ol>, so order is part of the semantics; each block title is an <h3>, which lets a screen reader jump block to block. The Saturday / Sunday switch follows the APG Tabs pattern; Lock day and Lock block are toggle buttons (APG Button, aria-pressed).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabRegenerate, Lock day, then each block's actions (or chevron under 720px), each leg's Directions link, then Add an errand.
← / →On the day tabs, moves between Saturday and Sunday.
Enter / SpaceActivates the focused action; Space toggles Lock.
EscCloses a block dialog and returns focus to the control that opened it.

Focus

Actions are revealed on :focus-within, so tabbing into a row shows them before the focus ring lands on the first one. The ring is drawn on the focused control, not the row. After Swap or Regenerate replaces a block, focus moves to the new block's title (tabindex="-1"), never back to the top of the page.

Labelling

  • Stop numbers carry visually hidden text: <span class="visually-hidden">Stop </span>2. The mock's aria-label="Stop 2" on a <span> is ignored by most screen readers.
  • Icon actions name the verb and the block: "Swap Lavender fields", "Why this: Lunch at La Marina", "Mark Costco run done". The chevron is "Details for Lavender fields".
  • An aria-label on a labelled day button starts with its visible label (2.5.3): "Lock day: Saturday", not "Lock Saturday".
  • Legs keep the mock's spelled-out aria-label ("Travel: 45 minutes, 52 kilometres to Lavender fields"); Directions says it opens a new tab.
  • The weather disc is decorative; the forecast is in the meta text.

Motion

The row tint and the action fade run over --durationFast. Under prefers-reduced-motion both are instant. Nothing in the itinerary moves on its own.

Touch

Row actions are 32px small buttons, the chevron is 32px, day buttons are 36px wide, Directions is at least 24px tall and the ghost row is 44px (--targetComfortable). All clear the 24px minimum (2.5.8).

Content

  • Times are 24-hour without a leading zero ("8:30", "13:30"). Durations in a block are compact: "30m", "75m", "1h 45m", "2h", or "open" for an evening left free.
  • Titles name the thing, with the place when it matters: "Swim lessons", "Lavender fields", "Lunch at La Marina".
  • Sub lines give the place and one reason, joined by a middle dot: "Terre Bleu, Milton · walk the rows". One line; it is clamped.
  • Day meta: date · high / low · stops · driving, in full words: "17 May · 22° / 14° · 3 stops · 1 h 36 min driving".
  • Legs: "45 min · 52 km"; the last one says "home". Directions only over 10 minutes.
  • Chips: one word, sentence case: "Commitment", "Locked", "Errand", "Day highlight".
