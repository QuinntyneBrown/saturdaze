Component · Data display

Map

The map shows the shape of one day: home in Port Credit, the stops in order, and the drive that joins them. Its numbers are the same as the itinerary's, so "stop 2" means Lavender fields on both.

Overview

Use the map beside an itinerary to answer "where are we going, and how far apart is it?". It is a companion, never the only way to read the plan: everything on it is also in the ordered list of blocks.

  • Map (.map): a framed 4:3 drawing with land, water, roads, the route, a home marker, numbered pins and place labels, an Open map button and a legend with the totals and attribution.
  • Planner (.planner): the day and its map together. Under 1024px the map sits above the day; from 1024px it sits beside it and stays in view while the day scrolls.
  • Planner toolbar (.planner__toolbar): the Saturday / Sunday segments that pick the day.
The mocks draw an illustrative SVG. Production renders real map tiles with the same pins, route and legend (docs/specs L2-100); the tokens, numbering, legend and text alternative on this page apply to both.

Anatomy

  1. Land, water, roads.map__land, .map__water, .map__road with a .map__road-edge centre line. Quiet tints that stay behind the route.
  2. Home.map__home: an ink disc with a house glyph, where every day starts and ends.
  3. Route.map__route: 3px dashed brand line, in stop order, back home.
  4. Pin.map__pin: brand disc with a 2px surface ring and the stop number (.map__pin-num), 24px.
  5. Active pin and label.map__pin--active: 30px with an ink ring, for the selected block. .map__label: 12px place names with a surface halo.
  6. Open map.map__expand: a small quiet button pinned to the top right.
  7. Legend.map__legend: stops, driving time and distance; .map__attr holds the attribution, pushed right.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 1024px .planner is one column and .planner__map moves above the day (order: -1), full width at 4:3. A phone sees the shape of the day first, then scrolls into the times.
  • From 1024px the planner is two equal columns with a 40px gap. The map sits on the right and is sticky, 16px under the top bar (--layoutTopBarHeight + --spacingVerticalL), so it stays in view beside a long day.
  • The toolbar's segments grow to fill the row on a phone (flex: 1 1 13.75rem) and cap at 17.5rem from 720px.
  • The legend wraps: totals first, attribution on its own line when there is no room.
  • Open map gives the full map its own screen on a phone, where a 4:3 frame is too small to pan.

Theming

The map reads six component tokens, set on .map: --sd-map-land, --sd-map-water, --sd-map-road, --sd-map-road-edge, --sd-map-route and --sd-map-pin. The SVG classes only read these, so a re-skin or a dark theme never touches the drawing.

/* A quieter map for a printed weekend */
.map--print { --sd-map-land: var(--colorNeutralBackground1); --sd-map-water: var(--colorNeutralBackground3); }

In the dark theme land becomes deep leaf and roads take the dark surface. Water is a mix rather than a single token, color-mix(in srgb, var(--colorPaletteSkyForeground1) 28%, var(--colorNeutralBackground1)), so it follows the surface: a pale lake on the light theme, a deep slate-blue one on the dark theme. Pins switch to the light coral with a dark numeral. Production tiles should use a matching dark style.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The drawing is one <svg role="img"> with an aria-label that lists the route in order. Its pins and labels are not read separately. The map sits in an <aside aria-label="Saturday map">. The ordered list of blocks is the full text equivalent (1.1.1): it carries the same stops, numbers and drive times. The day switch uses the APG Tabs pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches Open map; pins are not tab stops. Select a stop from its block in the list instead.
Enter / SpaceOpens the full map.
← / →On the day tabs, switches between Saturday and Sunday and their maps.

Focus

Open map shows the shared ring. On the full map, focus starts on its close button and returns to Open map when it closes.

Labelling

The label follows one pattern: "Map of Saturday's route: home in Port Credit, 1 Swim lessons, 2 Lavender fields at Terre Bleu in Milton, 3 Lunch at La Marina, then home. Stop 2 is highlighted." Update it whenever a block is swapped. Text drawn inside the SVG is decorative to assistive technology because of role="img".

Water is a color-mix() scoped to .map, which the live pairs above cannot resolve, so it was measured in the browser: a pin or the route on water is 3.84:1 in the light theme and 4.11:1 in the dark theme (minimum 3:1), and a place label on water is 9.50:1 and 7.46:1. Water against land is 1.27:1 and 1.52:1; it is a background shape, not information, so it needs no minimum.

Motion

The illustrative map does not move. Production tiles must not animate pans or zooms on their own; a pan to the selected pin follows prefers-reduced-motion and jumps instead of gliding.

Touch

Open map is a 32px small button with space around it. Pins are 24px (30px active); they are a pointer shortcut, never the only way to select a stop.

Content

  • Legend: "n stops · driving time · distance": "3 stops · 1 h 36 min · 105 km".
  • Place labels: towns and landmarks the family knows ("Milton", "Port Credit", "Square One", "Lake Ontario"), not street names.
  • Attribution is required wherever tiles are used: "© OpenStreetMap contributors". The mocks say "Illustrative" in front of it.
  • The button says "Open map". Not "Expand" or "View".
