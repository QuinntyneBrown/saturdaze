Rating

Five stars that say how a past weekend went. On a Past card the stars show the rating and open the "How was it?" dialog; in the dialog they are a radio group that sets it.

Overview

Use rating only for the family's own verdict on a weekend they have had, from 1 to 5. It is not a review score for places, and it never averages several people. For a quick yes or no from each family member, use the vote row.

  • Display trigger (button.stars) sits on each Past card. It shows the current rating with a "4 of 5" label and opens D13 to change it.
  • Read only (span.stars with role="img") shows a rating where it cannot be changed, such as a shared weekend.
  • Input (.stars.stars--lg, role="radiogroup") is the "How was it?" dialog: five large ghost icon buttons, one per score.

Anatomy

  1. Filled starSolid star in --colorPaletteSunForeground3 (.icon--filled, or .is-on on an input button). One per point of the rating.
  2. Empty starOutline star in --colorNeutralStrokeAccessible. Outline versus solid is the cue, not colour alone.
  3. Value label (optional).stars__label: "4 of 5" in --typographyCaption1, 6px after the stars.
  4. Container.stars: inline row, 2px between stars, --borderRadiusSmall for the focus ring. A button, a span or a radio group.

Pick by what the person can do with it: open the dialog, nothing, or choose a score.

Sizes

Two sizes, tied to the variant. Display stars are 18px so a rating fits on a card row. Input stars are 40px inside 48px ghost buttons, because a parent taps them on a phone while the dialog is a bottom sheet.

Both themes

4 of 5
4 of 5
  • Not rated: a past weekend without a rating shows five empty stars and the label "Not rated" on its trigger. The trigger stays enabled; disabled is only for a weekend that has not ended yet.
  • Hover on an input star shows the ghost button tint behind it. It does not preview-fill the stars before it; the fill changes only when a score is chosen.
  • Checked fills every star up to the chosen one; the value sentence under the group updates ("4 of 5.").

Responsive behaviour

  • The input row is 5 × 48px plus gaps, about 250px, so it fits a 360px bottom sheet with room to spare and never wraps.
  • The display trigger keeps its size at every width; on Past cards it sits on its own line under the title.
  • Nothing changes at the 720px dialog breakpoint other than the dialog itself becoming centred.

Theming

The stars read --colorPaletteSunForeground3 for filled and --colorNeutralStrokeAccessible for empty, the value label --colorNeutralForeground2. Input buttons are ghost buttons and take their hover and focus from the button tokens. In the dark theme the sun fill lightens to sun 300 and the empty outline to umber 400, both keeping 3:1 against the dark card.

Re-skin by overriding the icon colour inside a scope, never by editing .stars: there is no per-component token yet, so the filled colour is set on .stars .icon--filled.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The input follows the APG Radio Group pattern: a role="radiogroup" with a name, five <button role="radio"> with aria-checked, and a roving tabindex so the group is one Tab stop. The display trigger is a plain button that opens a dialog. Read-only stars are one role="img" with a text alternative, so the five icons are not read one by one.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus into the group, to the checked star (or the first star if none is checked), and out again.
→ / ↑Moves focus to the next star and checks it; from 5 it wraps to 1.
← / ↓Moves focus to the previous star and checks it; from 1 it wraps to 5.
SpaceChecks the focused star if it is not already checked.
Enter / Space on the triggerOpens "How was it?"; focus goes to the checked star. Closing returns focus to the trigger.

Focus

Input stars are buttons and get the shared 2px --colorStrokeFocus2 ring with a 2px offset. The display trigger draws the same ring around the stars and label, rounded by --borderRadiusSmall.

Labelling

The group is named "Rating" in the dialog titled "How was it?". Each star is named by its score: "1 star", "2 stars" … "5 stars". The trigger names the action and the current value: "Rate this weekend, currently 4 of 5", or "Rate this weekend, not rated yet". Read-only stars say "Rated 3 of 5".

Filled and empty also differ in shape (solid against outline), so the rating reads without colour (1.4.1).

Motion

The ghost button tint transitions over --durationFast, zeroed under prefers-reduced-motion. Stars fill instantly; there is no bounce or sparkle.

Touch

Input stars are 48 × 48px with 2px between them, above the 44px comfortable target. The display trigger is the whole row of stars and label, 18px tall; it sits on its own line on the card with no neighbour within 24px (2.5.8 spacing exception). button.stars pads it to the 24px minimum.

Content

  • Value labels use "4 of 5", never "4/5" or "80%".
  • The dialog title asks plainly: "How was it?", with the dates and title as the subtitle ("10 – 11 May · Bronte Creek + Rec Room").
  • The sentence under the input restates the choice, with a short word for the top score: "5 of 5, a keeper." Keep it to one line; skip the flourish for other scores.
  • Unrated weekends say "Not rated", not "0 of 5".
