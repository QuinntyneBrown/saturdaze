Component · Auth and marketing

Auth card

Every signed-out screen is one card in one narrow column: the brand above, the card in the middle, three quiet links below. The card is either a short form (sign in, create account, new password) or a centred message with an icon (check your email, verified, link expired).

Overview

Use the auth layout for every screen a person sees before or while getting into Saturdaze: sign in, create an account, reset a password, verify an email, and the Saturdaze Admin sign-in and gate. It has no app navigation; the brand mark links back to the landing page. Anything a signed-in family does (editing members, changing the password from Family) uses a dialog instead.

  • Layout (.auth): full-height centring grid. .auth--stack pins the column to the top for flows that show several cards; .auth--inline drops the full height (documentation and galleries).
  • Column (.auth__col): 440px max, 24px between brand, card and footer.
  • Form card (.auth-card with .auth-card__form): left-aligned title and subtitle, fields, one large block primary button, and an alternative route (.auth-card__alt).
  • Message card (.auth-card__head--center with .auth-card__disc): centred icon disc, title and one sentence, then one or two large block buttons.
  • Email chip (.email-chip): shows which address a link went to, masked when the person is not signed in.

Anatomy

  1. Brand.auth__brand: 28px brand mark over an uppercase wordmark; links to the landing page (or Admin home).
  2. Card.auth-card: surface, --borderRadiusXLarge, --shadow16, 24px padding (32px from 720px), 16px between parts.
  3. Head.auth-card__head: __title (--typographyTitle1, the page's h1) and __sub (one sentence).
  4. Form.auth-card__form: fields 16px apart; .auth-card__row for "Remember me" and "Forgot password?".
  5. SubmitOne large block primary button (.btn--lg.btn--block), 48px.
  6. Alternative.auth-card__alt: the other route ("New here? Create an account"), centred.
  7. Footer.auth__foot: Terms, Privacy, Back to Saturdaze. Caption, --colorNeutralForeground3.

Sizes

One size. The column is --layoutNarrowMaxWidth (440px) wide at most; the card's padding steps from 24px on phones to 32px from 720px. Buttons inside are always large (48px) and full width, and fields are 44px (--targetComfortable), because these screens are mostly used on phones, often one-handed, often from an email link.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 720px the column fills the screen minus the 16px margin and the card has 24px padding; from 720px the card has 32px padding and the column stays 440px, centred.
  • .auth centres the column vertically in the viewport (100svh, so mobile browser chrome does not push it off screen). .auth--stack starts 40px from the top instead, for flows that may scroll.
  • .auth-card__row wraps: at 360px "Forgot password?" drops under "Remember me" rather than squeezing.
  • When the on-screen keyboard opens, the focused field scrolls into view; nothing in the card is fixed or sticky.
  • The email chip wraps anywhere (overflow-wrap: anywhere) so a long address never widens the card.

Theming

The layout sits on the page background (--colorNeutralBackground2) and the card on --colorNeutralBackground1 with --shadow16, which in the dark theme becomes a raised umber surface with a deeper, softer shadow. Disc tones (primary, accent, warn) and the spinner disc come from their components. The brand mark keeps the static coral (--colorBrandBackgroundStatic) in both themes; that is the only place that colour appears.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each screen is a page: <main class="auth">, the card title as the h1, a real <form> with a submit button so Enter submits and password managers recognise it. "Remember me" is a checkbox with role="switch" (APG Switch pattern). The sign-in error is a role="alert" banner; the verifying card is role="status".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabBrand link, fields in order, Remember me, Forgot password, submit, the alternative link, footer links.
EnterSubmits the form from any field.
SpaceToggles Remember me and the agreement checkbox.

Focus

On load, do not move focus into the first field automatically on phones (the keyboard covers the card); the h1 is read first. After a failed sign-in, focus the password field (now empty) so the person can retype at once; the alert announces the error. After "Resend", keep focus on the button and announce "Sent again" politely.

Labelling

Every field has a visible <label for>; hints and strength meters join the field's aria-describedby. Use the right autocomplete so browsers and password managers fill and save correctly:

FieldTypeautocomplete
Sign-in emailemailusername (the mocks use email; both fill, username pairs with the saved password)
Current passwordpasswordcurrent-password
New and confirm passwordpasswordnew-password, with a hidden username field on the reset form so the manager updates the right entry
Create-account and reset emailemailemail
Family nametextoff (it is a household label, not a person's surname)

Accessible authentication (WCAG 3.3.8). Signing in asks for nothing that has to be remembered or transcribed beyond what a password manager or paste can supply: never block paste in any field (including Confirm password), never add a CAPTCHA puzzle, and keep the email link flow as the alternative to typing a password. Error messages do not clear the email.

Motion

Only the shared control transitions (120ms) and the verifying spinner, which keeps turning under reduced motion (D29). Cards swap without animation between steps.

Touch

Fields are 44px, buttons 48px, and links in .auth-card__row and .auth-card__alt sit on their own line with at least 8px around them. The switch's label is part of its target.

Content

  • Titles are short and warm: "Welcome back", "Start planning weekends", "Check your email", "You are verified".
  • The subtitle says what this step does or how long a link lasts: "Sign in to see this weekend.", "The link works for 30 minutes."
  • Errors do not reveal which part was wrong: "That email and password did not match."
  • Mask addresses on signed-out screens (q••••••••n@example.com); show the full address only to a signed-in person (the admin gate).
  • The alternative route is a question and a link: "New here? Create an account", "Already have one? Sign in".
