Top bar

The bar across the top of every screen from 720px: the Saturdaze brand, the four places a family goes (Weekend, Ideas, Past, Family) and the account menu. Public pages use a lighter site bar at every width.

Overview

Saturdaze has three shells (ADR-009), and the top of the screen is different in each:

  • App top bar (.topbar) on the five signed-in screens, from 720px only. Below 720px the bottom nav carries the same four links and the top of the screen belongs to the page header.
  • Site bar (.sitebar) on public pages (landing, legal, a shared weekend) at every width: brand, "Sign in" and, on the landing page, "Create your account".
  • No bar on the bare auth screens (sign in, create account, reset password, verify email); the auth card carries the brand.

Admin has its own chrome: see sidebar navigation (ADR-014). For where each bar sits in the page and how it relates to page headers and tabs, see navigation and page structure.

Anatomy

  1. Brand.topbar__brand: the 26px brand mark and "Saturdaze" in --typographyTitle3 bold. Links to Weekend (app) or the landing page (site bar).
  2. Primary links.topbar__nav inside <nav aria-label="Primary">; each .topbar__link is 40px tall, 14px semibold, --colorNeutralForeground2.
  3. Current-page underlineA 2px --colorBrandStroke1 bar under the link with aria-current="page"; the label also darkens to --colorNeutralForeground1.
  4. Actions.topbar__actions, pushed right: the account avatar button that opens the account menu (D26).
  5. Bar64px (--layoutTopBarHeight), sticky, transparent at the top of the page; the content sits in a .container so it lines up with the page below.

Variants

App top bar

Signed-in screens from 720px. Exactly four links in the same order as the bottom nav. The examples use .topbar--static, a documentation modifier that shows the bar at any width and keeps it in the flow.

Scrolled

Once the page has scrolled more than 4px, the bar gets data-scrolled: an 86% canvas fill with a 10px backdrop blur and --shadow2, so content passing underneath stays readable. Back at the top it returns to transparent.

Sizes

The top bar and site bar have one height, 64px (--layoutTopBarHeight), at every width.

  • Current uses two cues: the darker label and the 2px coral underline. Colour alone would fail 1.4.1.
  • Scrolled is a bar state, not a link state: data-scrolled is set by a scroll listener (the product's sdScrolled directive) once scrollY > 4.
  • There is no disabled link. A destination the family cannot use yet is left out, not greyed.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 720px the app top bar is display: none; the bottom nav takes over and the account menu is reached through the Family tab.
  • From 720px the top bar shows, sticky at the top, and the bottom nav hides. One switch point, so a page never shows both.
  • The bar's content sits in .container, so the brand lines up with the page header below at every width (16 / 24 / 32px margins).
  • Site bar under 380px: the gap tightens to 8px and "Sign in" hides; the brand and the call to action stay, and the landing hero repeats "Sign in". This applies only to bars with a call to action (.sitebar--cta, which sd-sitebar sets with cta); legal and shared-weekend bars keep "Sign in" at every width.
  • Labels never truncate. Four one-word links plus brand and avatar fit at 720px with room to spare; a fifth destination belongs somewhere else (see do and don't).
  • The sticky bar covers the top 64px, so pages set scroll-padding-top to the bar height plus 16px; anchored headings and focused fields are not hidden under it.

Theming

The bars read alias tokens only and have no component tokens: links use --colorNeutralForeground2 at rest, --colorNeutralForeground1 on hover and when current, --colorSubtleBackgroundHover behind a hovered link, and --colorBrandStroke1 for the underline. The scrolled fill mixes --colorNeutralBackground2 at 86% with transparency, so it follows whichever canvas the theme sets.

In the dark theme the canvas is umber 900; the scrolled bar is the same umber with blur, the underline lightens to coral 300 and the shadow deepens. Nothing in the bar is a literal colour (the mock's 1px hairline and the avatar tones are tokens).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The bar is a <header> placed directly in <body>, so it is the page's banner landmark. The links sit in <nav aria-label="Primary">; the bottom nav uses the same label because only one of the two is ever rendered. These are plain links, not an ARIA menubar: Tab moves between them. The account button follows the APG Menu Button pattern. See also landmark regions.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabSkip link, brand, the four links, the account button, then the page.
EnterFollows a link; opens the account menu.
Space / ↓Opens the account menu and focuses its first item.
EscCloses the account menu and returns focus to the avatar button.

Focus

Every link and the avatar button take the shared 2px --colorStrokeFocus2 ring with a 2px offset on :focus-visible. Because the bar is sticky, a focused element further down the page could slide under it (2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured); scroll-padding-top: calc(var(--layoutTopBarHeight) + var(--spacingVerticalL)) on html prevents that.

Labelling

The nav is named "Primary". The current page has aria-current="page", which screen readers announce as "current page". The avatar button is named "Account menu" (it shows only an initial or a photo) and carries aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded. The brand mark is decorative; the brand link's name is "Saturdaze".

Motion

The scrolled fill and shadow fade in over --durationNormal (220ms); link hover over --durationFast. Under prefers-reduced-motion both durations are zeroed, so the change is instant. The bar never slides away on scroll: hiding navigation on scroll makes it hard to find and moves the target under the pointer.

Touch

The top bar appears only from 720px, which includes touch tablets. Links are 40px tall with 4px between them; the avatar is 32px. Both clear the 24px minimum (2.5.8). Hover tint applies only under (hover: hover).

Content

  • Link labels are single nouns, the same words as the bottom nav and the page titles: Weekend, Ideas, Past, Family. A page called "Family" is never reached through a link called "Profile".
  • Order is fixed: Weekend, Ideas, Past, Family. It never changes with the current page.
  • Public pages say "Sign in" (not "Log in") and "Create your account" (not "Sign up").
  • The brand is always "Saturdaze" with the mark; no tagline in the bar.
