Component · Auth and marketing

Hero

The hero is the first thing a parent sees on saturdaze.app: what Saturdaze does in five words, one sentence of how, one button to start, and a picture of a real Saturday. The three steps below it answer "what do I have to do?".

Overview

Use the hero once, at the top of the landing page. It is the only place the display type size (--typographyDisplay, --fontSizeHero900) appears. Inside the app, a page starts with a page header; a first-run screen uses the warm empty state, which shares the hero's warm wash.

  • Hero (.hero): a large card with the warm wash. Copy on the left, preview on the right from 1024px; stacked below that.
  • Browser frame (.browser-frame): a simplified browser window around a scaled preview of the Weekend page, so the picture reads as "this is the app", not as a photo.
  • Steps (.steps / .step): three numbered cards, one row from 720px, stacked on phones.

Anatomy

  1. Container.hero: --colorBrandBackgroundGradient over the card surface, --borderRadiusXLarge, padding 40 / 56 / 64px (D16).
  2. Eyebrow.hero__eyebrow: uppercase caption, wide tracking, --colorNeutralForeground2.
  3. Title.hero__title: the page's h1, --typographyDisplay (700 weight, D14), line height 1, balanced wrap.
  4. Lede.hero__lede: 17px (--fontSizeBase500), 540px max, one or two sentences.
  5. Call to action.hero__cta: one large primary link-button and a .hero__note beside it.
  6. Note.hero__note: the reassurance that removes the last doubt ("Free during the beta.").
  7. Alternative.hero__alt: "Already have an account? Sign in" for returning parents.

The preview (.hero__preview) holds a .browser-frame: __bar (36px, three 8px __dots, a __url pill) and __view (the clipped preview). Each step has __num ("01", brand foreground, bold caption), __title (--typographyTitle3, bold) and __body.

How it works: steps

How a weekend comes together

Three small moments and the logistics disappear.

  1. 01

    Tell us who is in the family

    Names, ages and what each kid is into. A minute of setup, paid back every weekend.

  2. 02

    Lock what does not move

    Saturday swim, Sunday church, the weekly workout. They are anchors, and the plan goes around them.

  3. 03

    Get a weekend every Friday

    A draft of both days at 6pm, weather-checked and drive-time-checked. Swap anything you do not love.

An ordered list: the order is the point. The numbers are visible text, so they also survive copy and paste.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 720px: one column. Copy first, preview under it, steps stacked. Padding 40 / 24px; the call to action and its note wrap onto two lines if they do not fit.
  • 720 to 1023px: still one column, more padding (56 / 40px); the three steps sit in one row.
  • 1024px and up: copy and preview side by side (1.05fr / 1fr), vertically centred, 56px apart; padding 64 / 56px.
  • The preview never scrolls: .browser-frame__view clips it, and it scales with its column, so no width causes horizontal scrolling.
  • The title uses text-wrap: balance and an explicit <br> between its two sentences; it never hyphenates.

Theming

The hero is the warm wash (--colorBrandBackgroundGradient) over --colorNeutralBackground1: coral 100 and sun 100 glows in light, coral 900 and sun 900 in dark. The browser frame and steps are plain card surfaces with --colorNeutralStroke2 borders, and the frame's bar is the sunken --colorNeutralBackground3. Step numbers use --colorBrandForeground1. The preview inside the frame uses the real Weekend components, so it follows the theme automatically.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-labelledby> named by the title, which is the landing page's only h1. The call to action navigates, so it is an <a> styled as a button, not a <button>. The preview is decorative and aria-hidden="true": it repeats what the lede says and contains no controls. Steps are an <ol> with h3 titles under the section's h2. No APG pattern applies.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSite bar links, then "Create your account", then "Sign in". The preview and steps are skipped.
EnterFollows the focused link.

Focus

Both links use the shared focus ring, which reads against the warm wash in both themes. Nothing inside the aria-hidden preview may be focusable; if the preview is ever made from live markup, add inert as well.

Labelling

The title's <br> is read as a pause between "Two days." and "Already planned."; keep the full stops so it is not read as one phrase. The URL in the frame is decoration, not a link.

The wash fades from coral 100 and sun 100 at the top corners to the plain surface, so the corner colours are the worst case for text. The note reads --colorNeutralForeground3.

Motion

None besides the button's 1px hover lift, which reduced motion removes. The preview is static; do not animate it into view.

Touch

The call to action is 48px tall. "Sign in" sits on its own line, 16px below, with the full line height as its target.

Content

  • Eyebrow: who it is for, in a phrase. "Weekends, drafted for your family".
  • Title: the outcome, two short sentences, under 30 characters. No product name; the site bar already says it.
  • Lede: how it works, with real specifics (swim lessons, church, the weather). One sentence, 25 words or fewer.
  • Call to action: "Create your account". The note removes the cost question: "Free during the beta."
  • Steps: a verb phrase title and two short sentences each; numbers as "01", "02", "03".
  • No superlatives ("the best", "effortless"), no exclamation marks, sentence case throughout.
