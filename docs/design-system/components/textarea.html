Textarea

A textarea takes a few sentences: what a suggested event is and who it suits, or why a suggestion was turned down. It starts three lines tall and grows as the person writes.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAngular: sd-text-input multilineNative <textarea>

Overview

Use a textarea when the answer is more than one line: a description, a note, a reason. For a title, a name or a URL use a text field, even if the value is long; a single-line input tells people the answer should be short. The textarea is textarea.field__input, so it shares the text field's border, focus, invalid, disabled and read-only states, inside the same form field wrapper.

  • Plain: grows with the content (field-sizing: content), from at least 96px (about three lines). The person can also drag the corner to make it taller.
  • With counter: when the server stores a limited length, a .field__foot shows the hint and a counter such as "212 / 500".

Anatomy

  1. LabelFrom the form field: visible, above, with an optional marker.
  2. Text areaBody 1 at line height 1.5, 10px top and bottom padding, 12px sides. Minimum 96px tall; grows with content.
  3. Resize handleVertical only (resize: vertical), so the layout never widens.
  4. Foot (optional)Hint on the left, counter on the right in tabular figures.

Sizes

One size, set by content rather than a class. The textarea starts at three lines (rows="3", minimum 96px), grows with what is typed in browsers that support field-sizing: content, and can be dragged taller. Use rows="2" for a short note; never fewer than two lines, or it reads as a text field. Width follows the column, like every field.

States

The same states as the text field. Hover and focus are forced with data-state.

  • Over the limit: let people type past it (then trim), rather than cutting them off with maxlength mid-sentence when the limit is a soft one. Mark the field invalid and say how much to shorten.
  • Read-only shows a submitted description to an admin: well fill, no border, selectable.

Responsive behaviour

  • Full width of its column at every breakpoint.
  • Growing with content keeps the whole text visible while typing on a phone; inside a bottom-sheet dialog the sheet scrolls, not the textarea, until the sheet reaches 88% of the viewport height.
  • The resize handle is for pointer users; on touch screens the auto-grow does the same job.

Theming

Identical to the text field: alias tokens for fill, value and placeholder, and --sd-field-border for the edge. The counter reads --colorNeutralForeground3; over the limit, the error line carries the colour, not the counter.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <textarea> (role textbox, multi-line); no APG widget pattern is needed.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabInto and out of the textarea. Tab never inserts a tab character.
EnterNew line. It does not submit the form; the person uses the dialog's button.
↑ / ↓, Home / EndMove the caret between lines and to the line ends.

Focus

The field's own ring (focus border and 3px halo), as on every text field.

Labelling

A visible <label for>. The limit is described once ("Up to 280 characters.") through aria-describedby. The visible counter is aria-hidden while there is plenty of room, so it is not read on every keystroke; when a tenth of the limit is left it joins the described-by list and a polite live region says "25 characters left".

Motion

The height follows the content instantly; only the border and halo transition (--durationFast, zero with reduced motion).

Touch

At least 96px tall, well over the target minimum. Text spacing overrides (1.4.12) only make it grow; nothing is clipped.

Content

  • The label names the thing: "Description", "Reason". Put the question in the placeholder or hint: "What is it, and who is it good for?"
  • Say who will read it when that changes what people write: "The reason is shown to the person who sent it."
  • Counters read "212 / 500" visually; announced, "288 characters left".
  • Over the limit, say what to do: "Shorten the description to 500 characters."
