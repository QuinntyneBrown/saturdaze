Pagination

Long Admin lists show one page of rows at a time. Under the list, the pager says which rows are showing and offers Previous and Next; above it, the toolbar searches and sorts and the count says how many match.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableAngular: sd-pager, sd-toolbarAdmin lists

Overview

Use the pager (.pager) under any list that can grow past one page: Admin's Places (312 places), Activity log, Review queue and Ingestion skips. A page holds 50 rows. The family app's lists are short (a weekend, this week's ideas) and never page.

  • The pager is Previous and Next only, with the range as text. See why not numbered pages.
  • The toolbar (.toolbar) sits above the list: a search field and one or two sort or filter selects. .toolbar__count under it says how many rows match ("8 places") and is a polite live region.
  • Do not page a list a person needs to see whole to decide (a weekend's blocks, a family's members). Do not use infinite scroll: a curator needs to come back to the same row, and a footer would never be reached.

For list and table layouts, see list, table and tables and lists.

Anatomy

  1. Range.pager__text: "21 to 40 of 312" in --typographyBody2, --colorNeutralForeground2, tabular figures; it takes the free space and pushes the buttons right.
  2. Previous and NextSmall quiet buttons with arrow icons on the outside edge; disabled at either end.
  3. Landmark<nav aria-label="Pagination">, 16px below the list.

Why not numbered pages

  • The lists are sorted for work: "Worst health first" puts what needs fixing on page 1. A curator works from the top and stops; nobody needs page 7 of a priority order.
  • To find a particular place, search is faster and stable; page numbers shift every time a photo is fixed and the sort reorders.
  • The range text ("51 to 100 of 312") already says where you are and how much is left, which is what page numbers were for.
  • A numbered row with ellipses does not fit beside the range at 360px, and its targets would be too small to share a row.

If a list ever needs random access (an audit by date), add a filter that narrows it (a date or a place select in the toolbar), not page numbers.

Sizes

One size: the pager uses small (32px) quiet buttons and 13px range text.

  • Loading: the pressed button shows its spinner (aria-busy="true") while the next page loads; the list keeps the current rows until the new ones arrive, then swaps them at once.
  • Disabled at the ends uses native disabled. The range text still explains why ("301 to 312 of 312").
  • Empty: when nothing matches, the list shows an empty state and the pager shows the empty text ("No places") with both buttons disabled; the count says "0 places".

Responsive behaviour

  • The pager is one row at every width: range on the left, buttons on the right. At 360px, "301 to 312 of 312" and both buttons fit in the 328px column.
  • Toolbar under 720px: one column, search above sort, each full width with 44px fields. From 720px: search takes the space and sort sits in a 240px column beside it, bottoms aligned.
  • After Next or Previous the view scrolls to the top of the list, not the top of the page, so the toolbar and count stay in view.

Theming

The pager adds only the range text colour (--colorNeutralForeground2) and the spacing above it; the buttons are quiet buttons and theme with them (see button theming). The toolbar is layout only; its fields theme with form field. No component tokens.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <nav> landmark named "Pagination" (name it after the list when a page has two, "Places pages"). Previous and Next are buttons that load the page in place. When the page number lives in the address (?page=3), they may be links instead (<a class="btn btn--quiet btn--sm" href>), and a disabled end becomes a <span> styled the same with aria-disabled="true", since a link cannot be disabled. No APG widget pattern applies; it is two buttons and text.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabPrevious, then Next; disabled buttons are skipped.
Enter / SpaceLoads that page.

Focus

After the new page loads, move focus to the list's heading or first row (with tabindex="-1" on the target) so a keyboard user starts reading the new rows, not the bottom of the old page. If Next becomes disabled while it has focus (the last page), focus must not drop to <body>; moving it to the list prevents that too.

Labelling

"Previous" and "Next" are enough inside a landmark called "Pagination". The range text is plain text, read in order. .toolbar__count has role="status", so "8 places" is announced politely after a search or filter change; do not also announce the pager range.

Contrast

Range text and count on the page
Button label on its fill
Button border on the page (D28)
Focus ring on the page

Disabled buttons (50% opacity) are exempt from contrast requirements; the range text carries the information.

Motion

Button transitions only (--durationFast). The new rows replace the old without animation, and the scroll to the list is instant under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Small buttons are 32px tall and about 90px wide, with 8px between them: above the 24px minimum. Admin is desktop-first; on a phone they are still comfortably apart.

Content

  • Range: "21 to 40 of 312", with "to" rather than a dash, so it reads the same aloud and on screen. Numbers use tabular figures so the text does not jitter between pages.
  • Buttons: "Previous" and "Next". Never "Older" / "Newer" (the sort decides what is older) or "More".
  • Count: the number and the plural noun of the list: "8 places", "1 place", "0 places".
  • Empty pager text names the list: "No places", "No activity yet".
