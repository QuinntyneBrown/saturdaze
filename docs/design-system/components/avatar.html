Avatar

An avatar stands for a person: Quinn's coral Q, Mae's sunny M, or a profile photo once someone adds one. Each family member keeps the same tone everywhere, so the family can spot themselves at a glance.

Overview

Use an avatar beside a person's name in a list or card, and on its own as the account button in the top bar. It is an identity marker, not a status: never use it for a place or an activity (use a disc for an icon in a circle).

  • Initials (.avatar with text) are the default: the first letter of the name, in the person's tone.
  • Person tones --q, --s, --e, --m, --indoor are assigned in the order members were added: the first member gets coral, then leaf, sky, sun and, for a fifth member, lavender (indoor). A member's tone never changes, even if someone earlier is removed.
  • Neutral (no tone) is for people outside the family: other submitters, admins in the admin console.
  • Photo (.avatar__img inside) replaces the initial once a profile photo is set (dialog D27, Family → Add photo).
  • Account button (.avatar--btn) opens the account menu from the top bar.
  • Group (.avatar-group) overlaps several avatars to show who is in (who voted, who is going).

Anatomy

  1. ContainerCircle (--borderRadiusCircular) sized by --sd-avatar-size, filled with the person's tint; clips the photo.
  2. InitialOne capital letter, bold, in the tone's foreground. 12px at the two small sizes, 13px default, 14px large.
  3. Photo (optional).avatar__img, object-fit: cover, fills the circle. The tint shows while it loads and if it fails.

Sizes

Pick by where the avatar sits, not by how important the person is.

sm: submitter lines and the admin nav. md: dense rows. Default: the top-bar account button. lg: Family lists and the account card. xl: the profile photo picker (dialog D27).

States

A static avatar has no states. The account button (.avatar--btn) shows a 3px --colorBrandStroke2 ring on hover (pointer devices only) and the shared focus ring on :focus-visible; while its menu is open it keeps aria-expanded="true".

Responsive behaviour

  • Avatars keep their size at every width; they never scale with the viewport.
  • The top-bar account button exists from 720px only. Under 720px the bottom nav's Family tab covers the account, and the Family page's account card shows the large avatar.
  • In the admin console the small avatar sits beside the admin email in the sidebar from 1024px; under 720px the email and Sign out hide and only the avatar remains.
  • An avatar is flex: none: in a row with a long email it never squashes into an oval; the text wraps instead.

Theming

Component tokens: --sd-avatar-size, --sd-avatar-bg and --sd-avatar-fg. Sizes override the first; person tones override the other two with the brand and palette tint pairs (--colorBrandBackground2 / --colorBrandForeground1, then Leaf, Sky and Sun Background1 / Foreground1). The photo picker previews at 96px by setting --sd-avatar-size locally (.photo-picker__preview).

/* A local re-skin: the photo picker preview is a 96px avatar */
.photo-picker__preview { --sd-avatar-size: 6rem; font-size: var(--fontSizeHero800); }

In the dark theme the tints become deep versions of each hue and the initials lighten, keeping every pair above 4.5:1. The group's separating ring uses the card surface (--colorNeutralBackground1), so it follows the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A static avatar is a <span>. The account button is a link to the account page or, where a menu opens, a <button aria-haspopup="menu" aria-expanded> following the APG Menu Button pattern (see menu).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the account button only; static avatars are not focusable.
Enter / SpaceOpens the account menu (or follows the link).
EscCloses the open menu and returns focus to the avatar.

Focus

The account button takes the shared ring (2px --colorStrokeFocus2, 2px offset), and it reads on its own, with or without the hover ring.

Labelling

  • Next to a name (lists, the submitter line, the account card): decorative. aria-hidden="true" on the avatar, alt="" on a photo inside it. Otherwise screen readers hear "Q Quinn".
  • Alone: the avatar is the only carrier of who it is, so label it. A static one gets role="img" and aria-label="Quinn"; the account button gets aria-label="Account menu" (the action, not the letter). The photo inside keeps alt="".
  • Group: label the group once (role="img" aria-label="Quinn, Sara, Eli and Mae") and hide each avatar, or follow it with the names in text.

Contrast

Neutral initial
Focus ring on page

The hover ring is a pointer affordance, not the only sign of the control, so it is not held to 3:1. The tints themselves are not required to contrast with the page: the initial carries the information.

Motion

The hover ring fades in over --durationFast; under prefers-reduced-motion it appears at once.

Touch

The account button is 32px, above the 24px minimum, with at least 8px to the nearest top-bar link. Avatars in lists are not targets themselves: the whole row is the link.

Content

  • One letter: the first letter of the name the family uses ("Mae", not "Margaret"), upper case. Two letters do not fit at 24px.
  • Without a name (an admin known only by email), use the first letter of the email.
  • Profile photos are of the person, cropped square, face centred; the picker says where they are shown (dialog D27).
  • The label of an account button names the action: "Account menu", never "Q" or "Profile picture".
