Empty state

An empty state turns "nothing here" into "here is what happens next". It names what is missing, says why in one sentence, and offers one action: plan the first weekend, clear the filters, go back to Family.

Overview

Use an empty state once you know a page or section has nothing to show. While you do not know yet, show a skeleton. When something broke, show an alert in place, or an error page if the whole page is gone. The empty and error states pattern covers which to pick.

  • First run (.empty--warm): the family has never had this thing. Warm wash, brand disc, one primary action. Weekend before the first plan.
  • Nothing yet (.empty): the place fills itself over time. Neutral disc, a quiet action that goes somewhere useful. Past weekends before the first Monday.
  • All caught up (.empty with .disc--accent): the work is done, and that is good news. Review submissions with an empty queue.
  • No results (.empty--compact): filters or a search hid everything. Sits inside the section, dashed border, "Clear filters".
  • No permission (.empty with .disc--warn): the content exists but is not for this account. Says who can see it and where to go instead.

Anatomy

  1. Icon disc.disc--xl (56px) with a 26px icon; tone matches the variant. aria-hidden.
  2. Title.empty__title, --typographyTitle2, a real heading (h2 on a page). Names the <section>.
  3. Body.empty__body, one sentence, --colorNeutralForeground2, 40ch max.
  4. Action.empty__cta: one button or link, centred.
  5. Note (optional).empty__note, caption, --colorNeutralForeground3: the passive path ("Or wait for Friday at 6pm").
  6. ContainerCard surface, --borderRadiusXLarge, 40 × 24px padding, 480px max, centred.

Sizes

Three sizes, chosen by how much of the screen is empty, not by importance.

  • Warm (.empty--warm): 560px max, 40px vertical padding, large primary button. The whole page is empty and this is the first thing the family sees.
  • Default (.empty): 480px max, 32px vertical padding, medium quiet button. The page's main content is empty.
  • Compact (.empty--compact): full width of its section, 24 × 16px padding, 8px gaps, dashed --colorNeutralStroke1 border, no fill, small button. One section of a page is empty and the rest still works (filters, other sections).

States

The empty state itself is static. Its states are the moment before it (loading, so it never flashes during a fetch) and the states of its one action. Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state.

  • Loading shows a skeleton of the real content, not the empty state. Showing "Nothing here yet" for half a second and then the list is the most common empty-state bug.
  • Action running: "Plan this weekend" goes busy, then the page moves to the generating state. "Clear filters" goes busy until results return.
  • When a filter change produces the empty state, announce it with a polite live region near the filters ("No ideas match these filters"); focus stays on the filter that was changed.

Responsive behaviour

  • Default and warm empty states fill the column on a phone (minus the 16px page margin) and cap at 480 / 560px from 720px, centred.
  • Padding stays 40 / 24px at every width; the title wraps (balanced) and the body caps at 40 characters a line so it reads as one thought.
  • The action is never full width on a desktop; on a phone the large primary button may fill the card if its label is long.
  • Compact empty states take the width of their section at every breakpoint.

Theming

The warm variant layers --colorBrandBackgroundGradient (two soft radial washes, coral and sun) over --colorNeutralBackground1. In the dark theme the washes switch to coral 900 and sun 900, so the card glows faintly instead of turning pink. The disc tones (--primary, --accent, --warn) come from the disc component. Everything else is neutral surface, stroke and foreground roles.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-labelledby> named by its title, so it appears as a region in landmark navigation and its heading in the outline. It is not a live region: a page that loads empty does not need an announcement. No APG pattern applies.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the one action. The disc, title and body are not focusable.
Enter / SpaceFollows the link (Enter) or activates the button (both), per button.

Focus

The action uses the shared focus ring. After "Clear filters", move focus to the first result heading or the results count, because the button that had focus disappears with the empty state.

Labelling

The title is a real heading at the next level under the page title (h2 under the page's h1). The disc is aria-hidden; never put meaning only in its colour. An action link that would be ambiguous out of context names its target ("Go to this weekend", not "Go").

Motion

None. The empty state appears without animation; it replaces a skeleton in place.

Touch

The action is at least 40px tall (32px for the compact small button, still above the 24px minimum) with no other target within 8px.

Content

Copy formula: what is (or is not) here as the title, why, and what happens next as one sentence, one verb as the action, and optionally the passive path as a note.

  • Sentence case, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
