Form layout

Form layout is how fields, groups and buttons stack into a form a parent can fill in one-handed: one column, labels above, 16px between fields, the main action where the thumb is, and errors collected at the top when something is missing.

Overview

Saturdaze forms are short: sign in, add an errand, edit a family member, suggest an event. Every one of them uses the same layout rules, whatever surface it sits on. The individual controls are documented on their own pages (form field, text field, select, radio group, switch); this page is about putting them together.

  • Single column (.auth-card__form, .dialog__body, .stack): one field per row, labels above, 16px apart. This is every form in the product.
  • Paired fields (.field--row): two short values that belong together side by side, such as Start and End, Cost and Ages.
  • Dialog form (.dialog__body + .dialog__actions): editing triggered by a button always opens a dialog (no inline forms in pages); the dialog panel is the <form>.
  • Form actions (.form-actions): the button row for a form on its own screen. Primary first in the source, stacked full width on phones, right-aligned from 720px.
  • Error summary (.error-summary): a list of what to fix, with links to the fields, shown at the top when a submit fails.

Forms set novalidate and show Saturdaze's own messages. The browser's bubbles disappear on the next tap, cannot be styled and do not match the product's voice.

Anatomy

  1. Error summary (after a failed submit).error-summary: a title that counts the problems and one link per problem. It takes focus when it appears.
  2. Field.field: label, control, then hint or error, 6px apart (--spacingVerticalSNudge). Required is the word "Required", not an asterisk.
  3. GroupA set of choices (here a segmented radio) labelled by a .field__label span through aria-labelledby.
  4. Paired fields.field--row: two equal columns, 12px apart, for two short related values.
  5. Actions.form-actions: primary first in the source. On phones it is on top and full width; from 720px it sits on the right.

Between parts the form uses one gap, 16px (--spacingVerticalL), whether the container is .auth-card__form, .dialog__body or .stack. Do not add margins to individual fields.

Variants

The layout depends on where the form lives. Pick by surface, not by how many fields there are.

Sizes

Form layout has no sizes. Width comes from its container (440px auth column, 480 or 520px dialog, 720px screen) and every control keeps its own 44px height.

States

A form is in one of five states: ready, invalid (after a submit with problems), submitting, failed (the server said no) and done. The action row carries most of it. Focus is forced here with data-state; real forms get it from :focus-visible.

  • Ready: Save is enabled from the start. The mocks disable it until required fields are filled (D10 Submit, D17 Add member, D18 Add commitment, D27 Save); here it stays enabled and a press with gaps shows the error summary instead, because a disabled button cannot say what is missing.
  • Invalid: validate on submit, not on the first blur of an empty field. Once a field shows an error, re-check it as the person types and clear the message as soon as it is fixed. Update the summary on the next submit.
  • Submitting: aria-busy="true" on the submit button only. Fields stay readable; a dialog may also set aria-busy="true" on .dialog, which dims its body and blocks edits.
  • Failed: a .banner--warn with role="alert" above the form; keep what was typed except passwords.
  • Done: a dialog closes and a toast confirms ("Mae saved"), or a success dialog follows (D9 "Added to Sunday at 9:15"). A screen form navigates back to where the person came from.

Responsive behaviour

  • One column at every width. Forms never go two-across at desktop; the container caps the line length instead: 440px for auth, 480 or 520px for dialogs (.dialog--wide), 720px for screen forms.
  • Paired fields (.field--row) stay two columns even at 360px, so use them only for short values: two times, two numbers, Cost and Ages, latitude and longitude. Anything longer than about 12 characters gets its own row.
  • Form actions under 720px: a column of full-width 44px buttons, primary on top (it is first in the source). From 720px: row-reverse, right-aligned, 40px; the primary is right-most.
  • Dialog actions under 720px: full width, 44px, primary moved to the top with order (the source keeps the mocks' Cancel-then-primary order). From 720px they right-align in one row, primary last, and a warn-text action moves to the far left (.dialog__actions-left). Under 720px the dialog is a bottom sheet with a drag handle.
  • Auth card padding is 24px on phones and 32px from 720px; the submit is always full width.
  • Every control is 44px tall (--targetComfortable) at every width.

Theming

Form layout owns spacing, not colour. Its gaps come from the spacing scale (--spacingVerticalL between parts, --spacingVerticalSNudge inside a field, --spacingHorizontalM between paired fields, --spacingVerticalS between action buttons) and its widths from the layout tokens. Colour comes from the controls and from the error summary, which reads the danger roles: --colorStatusDangerBackground1 fill, --colorStatusDangerBorder1 outline, a 4px --colorStatusDangerForeground3 edge and --colorStatusDangerForeground1 text.

In the dark theme the summary's tint darkens to a deep terracotta and its text lightens, so the pair keeps more than 4.5:1; the left edge keeps the summary recognisable even where the tint is close to the surface.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <form novalidate> with a real type="submit" button. No APG widget pattern applies; follow the W3C forms tutorial. A screen form is named by its heading (aria-labelledby) so it becomes a form landmark; a dialog form is named by the dialog title. novalidate turns off the browser bubbles only: keep required, type="email", autocomplete and inputmode for their semantics and autofill.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through fields and buttons in source order, which matches the visual order of the column.
EnterIn a single-line field, submits the form (needs a type="submit" button inside the form). In a textarea, adds a line.
Enter on a summary linkMoves focus to that field and scrolls its label into view.
Arrow keysInside a radio group or segmented radio, move the selection.
EscIn a dialog form, closes the dialog without saving and returns focus to the button that opened it.

Focus

When a submit fails, focus moves to the error summary (tabindex="-1"), which reads its title and links. Each link moves focus to its input (a small script calls focus() and scrolls the label into view, since a plain fragment link only scrolls). When the server rejects the form, focus stays on the submit button and the banner announces itself through role="alert". In .form-actions from 720px the source order (Save, Cancel) runs right to left on screen; keep only two buttons there so the order stays obvious.

Labelling

Every control has a visible <label for> above it. Groups use a .field__label span referenced by aria-labelledby. Hints and errors are tied to the control with aria-describedby, and an invalid control sets aria-invalid="true". Required fields say "Required" in text (.field__req); when most fields are required, mark the rest "Optional" instead (AD6 Reason). Placeholders are examples ("Costco run"), never the label.

Motion

The layout itself does not animate. Dialog forms rise in over --durationNormal, and the submit spinner turns until the request finishes. Under prefers-reduced-motion the rise is instant; the spinner keeps turning because it reports status (D29).

Touch

Inputs and phone action buttons are 44px tall, fields are 16px apart, and paired fields are 12px apart, so a thumb never lands on the neighbour. Labels are part of the hit area of checkboxes and switches ("Remember me").

Content

  • Labels are short nouns in sentence case, no colon: "Email", "Name", "Start", "Neighbourhood or address". A question works when it reads better: "What is it", "Which day".
  • Put format rules in a hint before anyone gets them wrong: "JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB", "Under 18 counts as a kid".
  • Error messages say what to do, in the same words in the summary and under the field: "Add a title for the event", "Enter an email address like name@example.com", "End must be after Start".
  • The summary title counts: "There is 1 thing to fix", "There are 2 things to fix".
  • The submit names the result and matches the title: "Add an errand" → "Add to weekend", "Add a family member" → "Add member", "Edit Mae" → "Save". "Submit" only for things that go to review ("Suggest an event").
  • Never blame: "That email and password did not match", not "Invalid credentials".
