Foundation

Motion

Motion in Saturdaze is short and quiet. It confirms that something happened (a hover, a lock, a dialog opening) and shows that work is under way. It never decorates, and it stops when the person has asked their device for less motion.

Overview

The mocks animate very little, and that is the point. A parent planning Saturday on a phone should never wait for an animation to finish before they can tap the next thing. Every transition in the product comes from four durations and two curves:

  • Fast (--durationFast, 120ms): hover, press and colour changes. Long enough to read as a response, short enough to feel instant.
  • Normal (--durationNormal, 220ms): something appears or changes position: a dialog rises, a switch flips, the top bar fills in when the page scrolls.
  • Spin (--durationSpin, 900ms): one turn of a spinner. It loops while work is pending.
  • Shimmer (--durationShimmer, 1400ms): one sweep of the skeleton highlight while a weekend loads.

Everything eases out with --curveEasyEase (fast start, gentle stop) except the spinner, which turns at constant speed on --curveLinear. Components never write a raw duration: under prefers-reduced-motion the tokens are zeroed, so every component follows without its own media query.

Easing

One curve does almost all the work. --curveEasyEase starts quickly and decelerates, so a change registers on the first frame and settles softly. Linear is only for continuous rotation, where any easing would read as a stutter once per turn.

Both at 220ms. Easy ease arrives sooner and lands softly; linear feels mechanical for a move.
TokenValueUse
--curveEasyEaseEvery transition and the skeleton sweep. Equivalent to an ease-out with a quick start.
--curveLinearSpinner rotation only.

Usage

Choreography

  • Motion follows the cause. The element the person touched responds first; anything it reveals (a dialog, a menu) follows. Nothing moves on its own except loading indicators.
  • One thing moves at a time. No staggered lists, no cascades of cards. When planning finishes the whole timeline replaces the skeleton at once.
  • Small distances. Lifts are 1px (buttons) or 2px (cards); entrances rise 4px; toasts leave by 8px. Nothing slides across the screen.
  • Enter on Normal, respond on Fast. Appearing takes 220ms; hover and press take 120ms. Leaving is the same or faster than entering, and a dialog that closes may simply disappear.
  • Animate paint, not layout. Transition colour, opacity, shadow and transform. The only layout property that animates is the progress fill's width.
  • Page loads are still. Pages, the timeline, cards and the map render in place; the only motion on arrival is a skeleton or spinner while data is on its way.
  • Tokens only. Write var(--durationFast) and var(--curveEasyEase), never 150ms or ease-in-out; a raw value escapes reduced motion.

Accessibility

Reduced motion

When the device asks for reduced motion, tokens.css sets --durationFast, --durationNormal and --durationShimmer to 0.01ms. Every transition and the dialog rise become instant, the skeleton's sweep stops (the skeleton also drops its animation), the indeterminate bar stops, and the docs' smooth scrolling turns off. --durationSpin is kept, so spinners keep turning: they are a status indicator, and a frozen spinner reads as a hung app.

@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) {
  :root {
    --durationFast: 0.01ms;
    --durationNormal: 0.01ms;
    --durationShimmer: 0.01ms;
    /* --durationSpin is kept: a spinner is a status indicator, not decoration. */
  }
}

The values in the tables on this page are live, so with reduced motion on they read 0.01ms. 0.01ms rather than 0 keeps transitionend and animationend events firing for any script that waits on them.

What this fixes

The mocks cut every animation to 0.001ms under reduced motion, which also froze the spinner on "Sketching Saturday and Sunday" (drift D29). Zeroing the tokens instead lets each kind of motion decide: decoration stops, status continues.

Other rules

  • Nothing flashes more than three times a second (2.3.1). No component flashes at all.
  • Loops only run while work is pending and always sit beside text that says what is happening (2.2.2, 4.1.3). Long waits switch from a spinner to a sentence; planning a weekend usually takes four to six seconds.
  • No parallax, no scroll-linked animation, no autoplaying media.
  • Motion never carries meaning alone: a switch that slides also changes colour and its checked state; a dialog that rises also moves focus.
  • Hover motion only applies under (hover: hover), so touch screens do not get stuck mid-lift.
