Pattern

Feedback and loading

Every action gets an answer a parent can see without hunting for it: the control changes, a toast confirms, a banner explains, or a dialog shows what happened. Every wait says what is happening and roughly how long it takes.

Overview

Feedback sits as close to the action as it can, and is only as loud as it needs to be. From quietest to loudest:

  1. The control itself changes state: a lock button is pressed, a vote fills, a filter chip turns dark.
  2. Inline: a field error, a .field__success line, an .inline-status next to a region.
  3. Toast: a short confirmation of something the person just did, often with Undo.
  4. Banner: a condition that holds until it is fixed (sign-in failed, the forecast is unavailable).
  5. Dialog: the result needs reading or a decision (D9 Errand added, D11 Submitted).

Loading follows the same idea: show the shape of what is coming when you know it (skeleton), a spinner when you do not, a progress bar when you can measure it, and a status row when the wait is long enough to need an explanation.

Structure

Saturday

17 May · 22° / 14° · Light breeze, good for outdoors

From weekend.generating. aria-busy="true" on main; the skeleton list is aria-hidden; the status row is the only thing announced.
  1. Start. "Plan this weekend" navigates to the generating state at once. The request is idempotent (ADR-003): re-posting returns the weekend already being planned, so a double tap or a reload is safe.
  2. Wait. Header actions are disabled (there is nothing to share yet), the status row explains the work, day headers show the forecast, skeleton rows hold the timeline's shape.
  3. Still waiting at 10 seconds. The status text changes once: "Still working. Busy weekends take a little longer." No percentages that are not real.
  4. Done. Skeleton rows are replaced by blocks in place, the status row is removed, actions enable, and the status region announces "Weekend planned". Focus stays where it was; nothing scrolls.
  5. Failed. The status row becomes a danger banner with "Try again" (which re-posts safely). The empty-state copy does not come back; the person already asked.

Responsive behaviour

  • Skeletons use the same grid as the content they stand for, so they change with it: one day column under 1024px, two from 1024px.
  • The status row spans the content column at every width; its text wraps, the spinner disc stays 40px.
  • Toasts sit above the bottom nav under 720px and in the bottom-right corner from 720px (see Notifications).
  • Phone dialogs show busy on the full-width button, which sits under the thumb.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Status text lives in a polite live region (role="status") that exists before the message is written into it; errors use role="alert" (4.1.3). Regions being replaced carry aria-busy="true"; skeletons are aria-hidden="true". A progress bar is role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow, min and max, labelled by its visible title.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips disabled header actions while generating; reaches Undo and Try again in toasts and banners.
Enter / SpaceActivates Undo or Try again.
EscDismisses the focused toast.

Focus

Loading never moves focus. When content arrives, focus stays on the element that had it; when that element is replaced (a skeleton becomes a block), focus goes to the region's heading. Optimistic rollback keeps focus on the control.

Labelling

Busy buttons keep their name; the progress text ("Saving Mae") is announced from a status region, not stuffed into the button. Spinners are decorative next to words; a spinner alone needs a visually hidden label.

Motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion the skeleton shimmer stops and transitions are instant; spinners keep turning because they are the only sign that work is still going (drift D29).

Touch

Undo and Try again are at least 24px tall inside a 52px toast; banner actions are 32px buttons. Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Say what is happening, in the family's terms: "Working through your locks, the forecast and past weekends." Not "Loading…".
  • Give a time hint when the wait is longer than a couple of seconds and you know it: "Usually four to six seconds."
  • Confirm with the object and the outcome: "Removed Costco run from Sunday", "Saved Mae", "Link copied". Past tense, no "successfully".
  • Failures say what did not happen and what is safe: "Could not lock Saturday. Check your connection." Then one action: "Try again".
  • Undo is always "Undo".
