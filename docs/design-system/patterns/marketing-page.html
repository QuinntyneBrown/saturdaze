Pattern

Marketing page

One static page that tells a family what Saturdaze does and sends them to the app. It speaks in the product's own voice, shows real screens and never asks for anything but a click.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableADR-018 · L2-150 to L2-154
  • Overview
  • Structure
  • Responsive
  • Accessibility
  • Content
  • Constraints
  • Sources

Overview

The marketing page lives at https://www.saturdaze.ca, outside the Angular app, as plain HTML, one stylesheet and one small script (marketing/). Every call to action is an absolute link to https://app.saturdaze.ca. It replaces the in-app landing page and its hero component.

Structure

  1. Sticky header. Wordmark, three anchor links (How it works, Inside the app, For families) and the primary button "Open the app". The header gains a blurred background once the page scrolls.
  2. Hero. Eyebrow, the two-line display title "Two days. Already planned.", a lede, the primary button "Create your account" with the note "Free during the beta.", and a line of text links. A desktop screenshot of the Weekend screen sits in a browser frame beside it from 1024px.
  3. Watch. "Saturdaze in one minute." with the brand video in a dark rounded frame; the browser's own controls, a poster, no autoplay.
  4. How it works. Three numbered steps on tinted cards (coral, sun, sky).
  5. Inside the app. Four tinted cards, each a phone screenshot cropped and tilted three degrees, a title and one sentence.
  6. For families. A bento of four cards with a tinted icon tile; the first and last span two columns from 1024px.
  7. Final call to action and a footer with Terms, Privacy, Sign in and the year.

Section headings are short, with the key phrase in coral (em), and each is followed by one sentence that ends in a "Try it free" link.

See it rendered: pages/marketing and pages/marketing.menu-open.

Responsive behaviour

Below 64rem the anchor links and the header button give way to a 44px round menu button; the menu covers the page under the header. The hero is one column until 64rem. Steps go to three columns at 45rem; feature cards to two at 45rem and four at 64rem. Gutters follow the app's 16, 24 and 32px. Nothing scrolls sideways from 320px up.

Accessibility

  • The page opens with the skip link; landmarks are header, two named navs, main#main and a footer outside it.
  • The menu button is named "Open navigation" or "Close navigation", carries aria-expanded and aria-controls="mobile-menu", and the closed menu is inert. Escape and choosing a link close it.
  • Sections reveal as they scroll into view; under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce they are all visible at once with no transition.
  • Every screenshot has alt text that says what the screen shows. The video has a label and the browser's controls, and never plays by itself.

Content

Speak to the family, not to one parent or one child. Name real anchors (swim lessons, church, the Costco run) instead of features. Keep each card to one sentence. Prices and plans are not mentioned while the app is free during the beta.

Constraints

  • The Content-Security-Policy is default-src 'self' with no inline script or style: tints are classes (tint-coral, tint-sun …), icons are an inline SVG sprite, and nothing loads from another origin.
  • Colours, type and spacing come from marketing/assets/tokens.css, generated from the TypeScript theme by npm run tokens (ADR-013). Never copy hex values into saturdaze.css.
  • The production stylesheet mirrors docs/mocks/styles/marketing.css rule for rule; the visual spec compares the two.
