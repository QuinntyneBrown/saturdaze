Pattern

Tables and lists

Families see short lists they can read at a glance: three commitments, four family members, a few ideas per section. Curators see long ones they work through. Both share the same rows, filters and pager.

Overview

A collection is shown as one of three things, and the choice follows what the person does with it:

  • List rows to open or recognise one item: family members, commitments, places, a sheet of options. Leading disc, avatar or thumbnail; title and sub line; a chevron when the row navigates.
  • Cards to browse and compare by picture: idea cards on Ideas, past weekends, submissions waiting for review.
  • Table to compare values across columns: the admin activity log on wide screens. Under 1024px the same data becomes an activity feed.

Around the collection sit, in this order: a toolbar (search and sort), filter chips, a live result count, the collection, and pagination.

When to use

QuestionAnswer
Rows, cards or a table?Rows when the title is enough to choose; cards when a photo or several facts decide it; a table when people compare one column across rows (who changed what, when).
Density?Family rows are 56px or taller with one sub line. Admin may use .table--dense (8 × 12px cells). Never dense on phones.
Filter how?Filter chips for a few known values (Outdoor, Indoor, Under 30 min). A select for a long list of values (Place, Administrator). Search for names.
Sort how?A labelled "Sort" select in the toolbar. Sortable column headers only in a real table, with aria-sort.
Paginate, load more, or show all?Family lists show everything (they are short by design) or a fixed number with "See all". Admin lists paginate with Previous and Next and a "1 to 8 of 8" range. No infinite scroll anywhere.
Select several?Only in admin, only when a bulk action exists. Families act on one item at a time.

Why no infinite scroll: the footer and pager stay reachable, keyboard focus is never lost under new rows, and a curator can say "it is on page 3".

Responsive behaviour

  • Toolbar: Search and Sort stack under 720px; from 720px Search takes the row and Sort is 15rem at the end.
  • Filter chips scroll sideways in one row on phones (.scroller-x, with faded edges) and wrap from 720px. They never wrap to a second row on a phone, which would push the list below the fold.
  • Cards: one column, two from 720px, three from 1024px (.grid-cards).
  • Tables: the table scrolls inside .table-wrap with a sticky header; the page never scrolls sideways. If a table would need more than one screen of sideways scroll on a phone, use the stacked feed instead.
  • Pager: the range text and buttons stay in one row; buttons keep their labels.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Lists are <ul> or <ol>; a row's whole area is one link or one button, never a link inside a clickable row. Tables are native <table> with a <caption> and scope on headers; sortable headers hold a button and the header carries aria-sort (APG sortable table). Filter chips are toggle buttons with aria-pressed in a labelled role="group".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSearch, Sort, each filter chip, each row, Previous, Next.
Enter / SpaceToggles a chip; opens a row; sorts by a column.
Space on a checkboxSelects a row; the selection bar's count updates.
Esc in searchClears the search (native behaviour of type="search").

Focus

Filtering and sorting keep focus on the control that was used. Next and Previous move focus to the list's first row (or its heading), not to the top of the page. A row removed by an action hands focus to the next row.

Labelling

The result count ("8 places") is a role="status" region, so it is announced after each filter change. Row checkboxes are labelled with the row's title ("Select Harbour Grill"). Thumbnails are decorative when the title says the same thing.

Motion

Row hover tints over --durationFast. Rows do not animate in or out when filters change; the count announces the change instead.

Touch

Rows are at least 56px; filter chips 32px with 8px between; the chevron is part of the row target, not its own button.

Content

  • Counts use the noun: "8 places", "1 place", "6 ideas". Ranges: "1 to 8 of 8".
  • Row titles are names; sub lines are facts separated by a middle dot: "Restaurant · 2 photos", "9 · Kid".
  • Filter chip labels are short adjectives or ranges: "Outdoor", "Under 30 min", "Ages 5+". The first chip resets: "All", "All kinds".
  • Column headers are one or two words in sentence case; units go in the header, not every cell.
  • Admin timestamps are exact and in UTC ("2026-10-06 14:02 UTC"); family-facing times are relative or local ("2 hours ago", "Sat 2pm").
