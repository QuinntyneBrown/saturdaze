Pattern

Forms

Saturdaze forms are short: a name and an age, an errand and a day, an email and a password. They sit in a dialog or on an auth screen, never inline on a page, and they tell a parent exactly what to fix.

Overview

A form is a form layout of form fields with one submit action. The rules that hold across every Saturdaze form:

  • One column. Fields stack in the order a person thinks about them. Two fields share a row only when both are short and belong together (Start and End, Cost and Ages).
  • Labels above the input, always visible. Placeholders show an example ("Costco run"), never the label.
  • Mark the exception. Most Saturdaze fields are optional, so required fields say "Required" in the label. No asterisks.
  • Validate late, re-validate early. Check a field when the person leaves it; once it shows an error, re-check on every keystroke so the error clears the moment it is fixed.
  • On submit, summarise. If anything is wrong, show an error summary at the top and move focus to it. Each line links to its field.
  • No inline forms in pages. A button that edits something opens a dialog (Angular CDK Dialog) or navigates to a screen. Family never turns its rows into inputs.

Saturdaze does not autosave forms. Every dialog form has an explicit Save, so a parent can back out with Cancel or Escape and nothing changes.

Anatomy of a form

  1. Title names the job and the object: "Edit Mae", "Add an errand". The sub line says why it matters ("Ages shape the picks").
  2. Error summary (only after a failed submit) sits above the first field.
  3. Fields in one column, 16px apart (--spacingVerticalL); label, input, then hint or error.
  4. Actions: Cancel and the submit verb; a destructive entry point (warn text) on the far left when editing an existing thing.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 720px a form dialog is a bottom sheet capped at 88% of the viewport height; the body scrolls, the header stays visible at the top of the sheet. Buttons are full width and 44px tall, primary on top.
  • From 720px the dialog centres at 30rem (--layoutDialogWidth) or 32.5rem for .dialog--wide forms; buttons sit in one row, right-aligned, primary last.
  • On screens, .form-actions does the same: stacked and full width on phones, a row from 720px.
  • .field--row pairs stay side by side at 360px because both fields are short; anything that would truncate at 360px goes back to one column.
  • Inputs are 44px tall at every width and use 16px text, so iOS does not zoom on focus.
  • When the on-screen keyboard opens, the focused field scrolls into view inside the sheet; nothing is pinned over it.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <form> with novalidate (the browser's bubbles are replaced by our messages), inside a CDK Dialog that follows the APG Dialog (Modal) pattern. Confirmations use role="alertdialog". Two or three choices use a radio group (APG Radio Group).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through fields, then Remove, Cancel, Save, then the close button; focus stays in the dialog.
EnterIn a single-line field, submits the form (same as Save). In a textarea, adds a line.
Arrow keysMove between options in a segmented radio.
EscCancels and closes the dialog, unless it is submitting.

Focus

On open, focus goes to the first field. After a failed submit, focus goes to the error summary; each summary link moves focus to its input. After a successful submit, the dialog closes and focus returns to the button that opened it. Server errors move focus to the banner only if the person was not already typing.

Labelling

Every input has a visible <label for>; "Required" is part of the label text, so it is announced. Hints and errors are linked with aria-describedby; when the error appears it replaces the hint in that list. Field groups (Which day) use a labelled radiogroup or a <fieldset> with a legend. Name, email and password fields set autocomplete (1.3.5); passwords allow paste and managers (3.3.8).

Motion

Border and ring changes use --durationFast; the dialog rises over --durationNormal. Both are zeroed under reduced motion. The Save spinner keeps turning: it is status, not decoration.

Touch

Inputs and dialog buttons are 44px on phones. The segmented radio options are 36px tall and fill the row. The "Required" tag and hints are text, not targets.

Content

  • Labels are nouns or short questions in sentence case: "Name", "Age", "What is it", "Roughly how long", "Which day". No colons.
  • Hints answer the question a parent would ask: "Under 18 counts as a kid.", "Set to the next hour."
  • Errors say what to do, not what went wrong: "Enter an age from 0 to 120." Never "Invalid input" or "This field is required".
  • Summary title counts the problems: "One thing to fix", "Two things to fix".
  • Submit is the verb for the job: "Save", "Add member", "Add to weekend", "Submit", "Sign in". Cancel is always "Cancel".
  • Full rules and the error formula live in Content; composed examples are in Content and tone.
