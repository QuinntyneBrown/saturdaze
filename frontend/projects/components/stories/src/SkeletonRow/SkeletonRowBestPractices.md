## Best practices

### Layout

- Stack three or four rows — roughly what a day of the weekend holds — under an `sd-status-row`.
- Put skeletons where the real blocks will land so nothing jumps when the plan arrives.

### Content

- Skeletons stand in for schedule blocks; don't use them for cards or forms with a different shape.

### Accessibility

- Rows are hidden from assistive tech and carry no text. Pair them with a `role="status"` message.
- Motion is disabled automatically for people who prefer reduced motion.
