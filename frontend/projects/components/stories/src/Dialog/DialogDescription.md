The panel every Saturdaze modal is drawn in: a title (and optional subtitle), a close ×, the body, and an action row. Below 720px it is a bottom sheet with a grip and rounded top corners; from 720px it is a centred modal. `sd-dialog` is presentational — real modals are opened with `@angular/cdk/dialog` (`Dialog.open(MyDialog, { panelClass: 'sd-dialog-panel', backdropClass: 'sd-dialog-backdrop' })`), which owns the backdrop, focus trap, Escape and the `role="dialog"` container. On first render the panel points that container's `aria-labelledby` at its own `<h2>`, and the × closes the injected `DialogRef` (and emits `closed`).

The host carries the `.dialog` block from `docs/mocks/styles/app.css` (`.dialog--wide` for the 520px form panel, `.dialog--specimen` when inline) and draws `.dialog__panel`, `.dialog__header`, `.dialog__title`, `.dialog__sub`, `.dialog__body` and `.dialog__actions`. Slots: the default slot is the body; `[slot=media]` takes the photo a dialog is about (first in the body, at most 240px wide); `[slot=actions]` takes the buttons (quiet first, then primary/danger — on phones the primary moves to the top); `[slot=actions-left]` takes a destructive "Remove" that sits on the left from 720px. `static` (or providing `SD_DIALOG_STATIC`) renders the panel inline, as in the stories below and the `/dialogs` gallery.
