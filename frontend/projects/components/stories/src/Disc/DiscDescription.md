An icon in a coloured circle. The `sd-disc` host is the disc and carries `.disc` plus its tone and size modifiers from `docs/mocks-v2/styles/app.css` (`.disc--sun`, `.disc--lg`, …); inside, an `sd-icon` named by `icon` is scaled to match (16px for `sm`, 20px for `md` / `lg`, 26px for `xl`).

Discs lead list rows (the commitments and errands on the Family screen), cards (a food card's fork) and auth / empty states (`xl`). They are purely decorative: the host is `aria-hidden`.
