A native checkbox with its label as the default slot, so the label can carry links ("I agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy"). Use it for consent and opt-ins inside a form.

The inner `<label>` carries the `.check` block from `docs/mocks-v2/styles/app.css`, with `.check__input` on the real `<input type="checkbox">` and `.check__label` around the projected content. It implements `ControlValueAccessor` over a `boolean`: there is no `checked` or `disabled` input, so bind it with `formControlName`, `[formControl]` or `ngModel`. `required` sets `aria-required`; pair it with `Validators.requiredTrue` on the control.
