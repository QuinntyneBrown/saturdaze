The centred empty and first-run state. `sd-empty` carries `.empty` from `docs/mocks-v2/styles/app.css` and renders an extra-large `sd-disc` (`icon`, `tone`), an `h2.empty__title` that labels the host, and an optional `.empty__body` paragraph.

Project the call to action into `[slot=cta]`; `note` adds a faint `.empty__note` line after it. `warm` switches to `.empty--warm`, the brand-gradient first-run variant used when a family has no weekend planned yet.
