A short list of actions: the account menu (Family settings, Sign out) and the Weekend "More" menu (Regenerate the weekend, Add to calendar). From 720px it is an anchored popover under its trigger, attached with CDK `Overlay`; below 720px the same items render with `sheet` inside an `sd-dialog` bottom sheet opened with CDK `Dialog`. In the app `MenuOpener` picks the form by width and resolves with the chosen item.

The host is the `.menu` block from `docs/mocks/styles/app.css` with `role="menu"` and `aria-label` from `label`; `.menu--sheet` switches to plain rows. Each `MenuItem` (`{ id, label, icon, sub?, tone?, href? }`) renders a `.menu__item` (`role="menuitem"`, `.menu__item--warn` for `tone: 'warn'`) — a `<button>`, or an `<a>` routed in-app when it has an `href`. The popover shows an `sd-icon`; the sheet shows an `sd-disc` and the item's `sub` line. `header` adds a `.menu__header` line (the account email). `select` emits the chosen item; Arrow Up / Down move focus between items.
