A catalog's photo coverage on the admin Photo health screen (L2-112). `sd-stat-card` carries `.card.stat` from `docs/mocks/pages/admin.html`: the catalog label, the "16 of 20" figure with a caption and a tinted bar, and a list of flag counts. Each count is a link that opens the Places screen filtered to those places, so every number on the home screen is actionable.

The bar's `role="img"` label reads the percentage aloud ("80% with a primary photo"); the figure and the links carry the rest.
