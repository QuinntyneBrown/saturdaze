## Best practices

### Layout

- One cover per screen, as its first element; it replaces the page header.
- Keep the actions in a row below the cover, not on the photo.

### Content

- Pass the cover's label as the media `credit` so the photo's source stays visible.
- Keep the summary to one sentence; the scrim is sized for about two lines.

### Accessibility

- The title is the page's only `h1`.
- The photo's `alt` describes the photo; the scrim keeps the overlaid text at 4.5:1 over light and dark images.
